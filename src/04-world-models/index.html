World Models & WAM

World Model 在机器人里主要有四种用法：当策略、造数据、做评测、做规划。本文从早期的视频预测讲到 World Action Model，最后接到隐空间规划和 Model-Based RL。

Four roles: policies, data generation, evaluation and planning. Connect video prediction and world-action models to latent planning and model-based RL.

1World Model 的四种用法Four roles for world models

World Model 要回答的问题是：如果执行这些动作，环境会变成什么样。它可以输出像素，也可以输出视觉特征、隐状态或跟任务相关的量，生成逼真的视频只是其中一种。对机器人来说，关键在于预测是否以动作为条件、在物理上是否可行、能不能在闭环里纠正，画面好不好看反而是次要的。

A world model predicts how the environment changes under actions. Outputs may be pixels, visual features, latent states or task-relevant quantities. Realistic video is one form, while action conditioning, physical feasibility and feedback are central to robotics.

同一个模型可以有多种用法，但每种用法要单独拿证据：预测的视频好看，不说明用它控制就稳定；离线排序和真机结果相关，不说明它能代替所有真机测试；生成的视频再多，也不等于有了正确的动作标签。

One model may serve several roles, but evidence does not transfer automatically. Visual quality does not prove stable control; offline ranking correlation does not replace arbitrary hardware tests; more videos do not necessarily provide correct action supervision.

2早期工作：用视频预测做控制Early approaches: video prediction for control

Visual Foresight：先预测候选动作的后果Visual foresight: predict consequences of candidate actions

Visual Foresight 把 action-conditioned video prediction 和 MPC 结合起来：先用机器人自己采的交互数据训练预测器，预测推动物体之后的画面，再挑出最接近目标图像的动作 [96]。它不需要人去标注哪条示范成功，但仍然需要知道机器人每一步做了什么动作。

Visual foresight combines action-conditioned video prediction and MPC, using robot interaction data to predict pushing outcomes and select actions toward visual goals [96]. Avoiding human success labels does not remove action conditioning: the predictor still needs the robot’s executed actions.

UniPi：先生成视频，再反推动作UniPi: imagine behavior, then recover actions

UniPi 根据文字生成一段视频当作计划，再用 inverse dynamics 把画面变化翻译成动作 [97]。这样“想看到什么”和“怎么做到”就分开了，但也暴露了一个关键问题：视频里可以演出成功，inverse model 却未必知道这台机器人要发什么指令才能做到。

UniPi uses text-conditioned video generation as a plan and inverse dynamics to recover actions [97]. It separates desired visual behavior from execution, exposing a bottleneck: a successful-looking video still needs commands that the current body can realize.

如果只根据任务描述生成视频，模型往往会画出数据里常见的成功画面，而不管机器人现在是什么姿态、有没有东西挡着、刚才有没有抓空。做控制必须分清“理想中的未来”和“从当前状态出发、执行这个动作后真的会发生的未来”。

Text-only generation can imagine a common successful outcome while ignoring the current posture, obstruction or failed grasp. Control must distinguish a desirable future from one achievable under the current state and actions.

The equation expresses a joint-model viewpoint, not a requirement to evaluate an explicit integral. Actions and future visual changes constrain each other. This differs from directly modeling , but joint prediction alone does not prove complete causal physics.

DreamZero：在视频模型上接出动作DreamZero: connect video priors to actions

DreamZero 以预训练的视频 diffusion 模型为骨干，同时预测视频和动作，训练数据来自多种不同的机器人 [14]。论文分别讨论了只用视频适配新行为、以及适配新机器人这两种情况，并做了系统层面的优化，让模型能闭环运行。看它的 zero-shot 结论时，要分别确认没见过的是任务、环境还是机器人本体，以及有没有用额外的适配数据。

DreamZero builds joint video-action prediction on a pretrained video diffusion backbone and heterogeneous robot data [14]. It distinguishes video-only behavior adaptation from adaptation to a new robot and optimizes the system for closed-loop use. For each zero-shot claim, identify unseen tasks, scenes and bodies, and whether adaptation data was used.

从 DreamZero 到 Dyna 和 GR00T 2From DreamZero to Dyna and GR00T 2

Dyna 主张先从大量人类视频里学运动和交互的规律，再用机器人数据把动作对齐；GR00T 2 的公开报告也走视频和动作联合建模的路线，并讨论了部署和适配 [18, 50]。两者都用了视频先验，但人类视频、配对动作、Post-training 和底层控制各占多少差别很大，一个 WAM 标签概括不了。

Dyna emphasizes motion and interaction priors from human video, grounded with robot actions. GR00T 2’s report uses joint video-action modeling and discusses deployment and adaptation [18, 50]. Both use video priors, but differ in paired actions, post-training and low-level control; the WAM label is not a complete method description.

4用 World Model 造数据：动作标签从哪里来Generating data: where supervision comes from

“用 World Model 造数据”其实至少指三件不同的事：给已有动作标签的 rollout 换个外观；生成新的场景和物体摆放，再用能执行的策略跑出轨迹；或者直接生成行为视频，再从中反推动作。第一种要保证换外观之后标签依然成立；后两种还需要额外检查动作和物理是否合理。

Data generation can alter the appearance of labeled rollouts, build new executable scenes, or synthesize behavior videos and recover actions. Appearance changes must preserve labels; new scenes and behavior videos need additional action and physics validation.

1X 的 video-to-action 路线先生成视频，再用 inverse dynamics 把它变成机器人动作 [53]。NVIDIA 的数据管线则把仿真、视频生成和真机数据分别用在训练的不同环节 [7, 50]。不管用哪套系统，都应该记清楚每一个动作标签是从哪来的：真实指令、模拟器、inverse model，还是生成器自己。

1X connects generated behavior videos to robot actions through inverse dynamics [53]. NVIDIA’s pipelines use simulation, generation and real robot data at different stages [7, 50]. Track whether each action label comes from actual commands, a simulator, an inverse model or the generator itself.

“一百万小时视频”说的是输入有多大，不等于机器人有一百万小时的有效经验。更有意义的对比是：在真实动作数据一样多的前提下，加了生成数据以后，新场景的成功率有没有提高，恢复成本有没有降低，又有没有冒出新的失败方式。

A million hours of video measures input scale, not an equivalent amount of robot experience. A stronger comparison holds real-action data fixed and measures whether generated data improves new-scene success and recovery, including newly introduced failure modes.

5用 World Model 做评测：排序靠不靠得住World-model evaluation: reliability of policy rankings

1X 的 “Evaluating Bits, not Atoms” 把候选策略放进学出来的 World Model 里评估，再看评估结果和真机表现对不对得上 [54]。好处是省下大量真机测试；难点在于，面对没见过的策略和接触状态时，这个学出来的环境还靠不靠得住。

1X’s “Evaluating Bits, not Atoms” evaluates candidate policies in a learned model and studies agreement with hardware outcomes [54]. It can reduce physical testing cost, but depends on model validity under new policies and contacts.

平均预测误差小，并不保证在决定两个策略高下的关键接触上也预测得准；排序对得上，也不代表成功率的绝对值准确。一旦有人开始针对这个评估模型反复调策略，策略就可能钻模型的漏洞，评估本身也就失真了。所以要定期用独立的真机测试重新校准，并把模型内外结果不一致的案例保留下来。

Small average prediction error can hide mistakes at the contacts that distinguish policies. Ranking correlation does not ensure calibrated success probabilities. Repeated optimization against the evaluator can exploit model flaws and change its input distribution. Recalibrate with independent hardware trials and retain disagreements.

6隐空间规划：不必把每个像素都画出来Latent planning without reconstructing every pixel

DINO-WM 与 V-JEPA 2-ACDINO-WM and V-JEPA 2-AC

DINO-WM 预测的是 DINOv2 的空间 patch features，测试时优化动作序列，让预测出的特征尽量接近目标图像的特征 [75]。这样既保留了空间结构，又不用逐像素去画背景细节；代价是，特征空间里的“距离”是否真的反映了操作所需的几何关系，只能靠具体任务来验证。

DINO-WM predicts spatial DINOv2 patch features and optimizes action sequences toward goal-image features at test time [75]. This preserves spatial structure without reconstructing every pixel, but the usefulness of feature distance for manipulation must be validated.

V-JEPA 2 先从视频里学表征，再用带动作的机器人数据做 Post-training，得到 V-JEPA 2-AC，用来做 action-conditioned prediction 和规划 [76]。这里的 “unlabeled video” 指不需要人工标注语义，并不是说控制阶段完全没用机器人动作数据。视觉 Pretraining 和控制适配用的是两种不同的监督。

V-JEPA 2 learns video representations; V-JEPA 2-AC is post-trained with robot action data for prediction and planning [76]. “Unlabeled video” does not mean robot action records are absent from control adaptation. Visual pretraining and action conditioning supply distinct supervision.

这两条线都属于传统的 Model-Based RL。状态预测得越逼真，控制不一定越好：模型容量有限，重要的是它有没有抓住和任务相关、动作能影响的那部分变化，以及 planner 会把模型误差放大到什么程度。

These connect to model-based RL. More realistic prediction need not improve control if finite capacity misses task-relevant changes. Evaluate what the model preserves and how planning amplifies its errors.

读这类工作，先弄清三件事：模型预测的是什么，以什么动作为条件，最后怎么选动作。再看训练数据里有没有目标机器人，评测是不是真的在闭环里做的。把画面质量、任务进度和完整成功分开报告，才不会把“想象中成功了”当成“真的做成了”。

First identify the predicted variables, action conditioning and action-selection mechanism. Then inspect target-body data and whether evaluation is closed loop. Separate visual quality, task progress and full success to distinguish successful imagination from successful execution.
