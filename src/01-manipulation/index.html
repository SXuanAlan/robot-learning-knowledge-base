1从操作策略到 Action ExpertFrom manipulation policies to action experts

本页的主线是 Manipulation Policies & Skills：给定观测和任务，怎样生成机器人能执行、又能根据反馈修正的动作。ALOHA 和 UMI 改变的主要是数据和执行接口；ACT、Diffusion 和 Flow 改变的是动作分布的表示方式；HIL-SERL 和 SGS 改变的是学习用的反馈。它们是同一个系统里可以搭配的不同部件，而不是互相替代的选项。

This page centers on manipulation policies and skills: producing executable actions from observations and task context, with feedback during execution. ALOHA and UMI mainly shape data and execution interfaces; ACT, diffusion and flow shape action distributions; HIL-SERL and SGS shape learning feedback. These choices compose rather than compete on a single axis.

Manipulation 难就难在接触：差几毫米的位姿、摩擦稍有变化、松手晚一点，结果都可能完全不同。所以不能只比 policy 网络，还要同时讲清楚数据从哪来、模型输出什么、控制器又怎样把输出变成真实的接触。

Manipulation is sensitive to contact: small pose errors, friction changes or release delays can change the outcome. Comparing networks alone is insufficient. Specify where data comes from, what the model predicts and how a controller turns that prediction into contact.

完整策略与动作专家的边界Full policies and action experts

单独使用的 ACT 或 Diffusion Policy，直接从图像和 Proprioception 生成动作，本身就是一个完整的任务策略。接上 VLM 之后，这个连续动作模块改为接收视觉、语言和状态的表示，再输出 action chunk，就成了更大系统里的 Action Expert，π₀ 的 flow-matching action expert 就是这种情况 [5]。这并不是每种方法都要走的升级路线，“expert”也不是说它只能做一个任务。

A standalone ACT or Diffusion Policy can map images and proprioception directly to actions as a complete task policy. Inside a larger VLA, a continuous-action module can instead condition on visual, language and state representations and generate action chunks as an action expert. The flow-matching action expert in π₀ is one example [5]. This is not a mandatory progression, nor does “expert” imply a single task.

2空间动作表示：从 Transporter 到 RVT-2Spatial action representations: Transporter to RVT-2

操作策略的发展不只有“MLP → Transformer → Diffusion”这一条线。另一条线是让动作和几何对齐：动作在哪个坐标系里输出，粒度多粗，底层规划器分担多少工作。这些选择直接决定了一个方法适合做桌面搬运、精细插装，还是持续接触的任务。

Manipulation does not follow a single MLP-to-transformer-to-diffusion progression. Another lineage aligns actions with geometry: coordinate frame, output granularity and the work delegated to a motion planner. These choices shape suitability for pick-and-place, precise insertion and continuous contact.

Transporter 与 CLIPort：把空间结构写进策略Transporter and CLIPort: spatial structure in the policy

Transporter Networks [98] 把抓取点附近的特征和整个场景的特征做匹配，预测东西该放到哪，相当于把空间平移关系直接写进了网络结构。CLIPort [99] 在此基础上加了一路 CLIP 语义：语言负责回答“拿哪个”，空间特征负责回答“在哪抓、放哪”。和直接回归一串关节角相比，这条路线把问题变成了有结构的空间决策；代价是能做什么，受限于事先选定的动作原语和控制接口。

Transporter Networks [98] correlate features near a selected grasp with scene features to predict placement, exploiting spatial structure. CLIPort [99] combines a CLIP semantic stream with a Transporter spatial stream: language selects what to manipulate while geometry localizes actions. This structures the decision problem rather than directly regressing joint trajectories, but execution still depends on the chosen action primitives and controllers.

PerAct 与 RVT-2：体素、多视图与操作精度PerAct and RVT-2: voxels, multiple views and precision

PerAct [100] 把 RGB-D 重建成体素，以语言和 3D patch 为条件输入 Perceiver，预测末端的平移、旋转、夹爪开合和是否允许碰撞，再交给运动规划器执行。这种“在空间里检测动作”的做法，保留了观测和动作之间的空间对应，但精度和开销都受体素分辨率和工作空间大小影响。RVT-2 [101] 改用多视图表示，并用由粗到细的定位提升精细操作；它解决的是怎样更高效地找到下一个目标位姿，而不是怎样生成一整段连续接触的动作。

PerAct [100] encodes RGB-D voxels and language with a Perceiver, predicts an end-effector pose, gripper state and collision flag, and relies on motion planning for execution. Voxel size trades precision against workspace and compute. RVT-2 [101] builds on multi-view representations with coarse-to-fine localization for precise manipulation. This predicts spatial targets efficiently; it is a different interface from generating a dense contact-rich trajectory.

比较实验时不能只抄 success rate。如果一个方法用了深度和相机标定、靠关键帧加规划器，另一个只看 RGB、直接输出高频动作，那两者的观测和执行方式本来就不同。这些条件应该和模型一起列出来。

A success-rate comparison is incomplete when one policy receives calibrated depth and uses planned keyframes while another sees RGB and emits dense actions. Report observations and execution backends alongside architecture.

3ALOHA 与 ACT：采集系统与动作序列学习ALOHA and ACT: collection systems and action-sequence learning

ALOHA：低成本精细双臂遥操作ALOHA: low-cost fine bimanual teleoperation

最初的 ALOHA 是一套 leader–follower 双臂 Teleop 系统：人摆动两条 leader 臂，两条 follower 臂在真实环境里跟着做；四路 RGB 和 follower 的关节状态作为 observation，leader 的关节目标作为 action label [2]。要记录的是“下达的目标”，而不是“实际到达的位置”，尤其是碰到阻挡时，两者会差得很远。

Original ALOHA uses two leader arms and two follower arms. The human moves the leaders, followers interact with the world, and observations combine four RGB views with follower joint states. Leader joint targets provide action labels [2]. Commands and achieved states differ, especially under contact, so recording only achieved positions loses the intended action.

ALOHA 的贡献在于：装置容易复现，leader 臂操作手感好，夹爪设计合理，相机视野清楚。ALOHA 和 ACT 不是一回事：同一套数据可以训练别的模型，ACT 也可以用在别的平台上。原论文研究的是固定场景下的单任务学习，后来 Mobile ALOHA、ALOHA 2 和大模型的能力，不应算到原版头上。

ALOHA contributes a reproducible apparatus, ergonomic teaching, gripper design and observability. It is distinct from ACT: the data can train other models, and ACT can run on other platforms. Original single-task experiments should not inherit capabilities from Mobile ALOHA, ALOHA 2 or later large models.

ACT：Action Chunking 与闭环执行ACT: action chunking and closed-loop execution

ACT 用 transformer 并行生成未来 joint-position targets，并用 CVAE latent 在训练时吸收示范的变化。Posterior encoder 会看到真实 future actions；部署时没有这些信息，因此丢弃该 encoder、固定 。原版 rollout 不是每个 chunk 都随机采一个 latent。

ACT uses a transformer to predict future joint targets in parallel. A CVAE latent absorbs variation in demonstrations during training. The posterior encoder sees future expert actions; deployment discards it and sets . Original rollouts do not randomly resample a latent for every chunk.

ALOHA 2：提高采集可靠性与可扩展性ALOHA 2: collection reliability and scalability

ALOHA 2 [108] 改进了夹爪、重力补偿、操作体验和硬件耐用性，还提供了经过系统辨识的 MuJoCo 模型。它的意义在于数据基础设施：每天能稳定采多久，要不要经常修，操作者做精细动作顺不顺手，都会影响最后采到什么样的数据。它不是 ACT 的新损失函数；硬件和策略的版本应该分开记录。

ALOHA 2 [108] improves grippers, gravity compensation, ergonomics and robustness, and provides a system-identified MuJoCo model. Reliable operating hours, maintenance and operator effort shape the resulting dataset. It is a collection-platform improvement, not a new ACT loss; track hardware and policy versions separately.

4Diffusion Policy：生成动作分布Diffusion Policy: generating action distributions

BC 的一大难点是多峰：同一个 observation 下，可以有好几种合理的抓法、绕行方向和操作节奏，把它们平均成一条轨迹，结果可能哪种都不是。Diffusion Policy 以观测历史 O 为条件，从高斯噪声一步步生成一整段动作 A，能表达多种各自连贯的行为模式 [1]。

BC faces multimodality: one observation can admit different grasps, routes and timings. Averaging them can create an invalid trajectory. Diffusion Policy conditions on observation history O and generates an action sequence A from Gaussian noise, representing multiple coherent behaviors [1].

训练时随机加噪，让网络预测噪声；推理时从纯噪声出发，反复调用网络把动作还原出来。原论文同时试了 temporal U-Net 和 time-series transformer：前者带有时间卷积的归纳偏置，后者用注意力建模序列。Diffusion 是一种训练和生成的框架，并不绑定某种网络结构。

Training corrupts expert actions and predicts the noise; inference repeatedly refines an initial noise sample. The paper studies temporal U-Nets and time-series transformers. Diffusion specifies a generative learning framework rather than one mandatory architecture.

原方法建模 ，并不要求同时生成未来图像。每次预测 步、执行 步后重新查询；视觉编码可以在同一轮去噪内复用。论文真机实验使用 DDIM 将训练的 100 个 diffusion steps 与推理的 10 steps 分开 [1, 59]，说明“扩散一定要运行全部训练步数”并不成立。

The original model learns , without requiring future-image generation. It predicts steps and executes before replanning, reusing observation features across denoising iterations. Its real-robot setup uses DDIM with 100 training steps and 10 inference steps [1, 59], so the training schedule does not force an identical inference budget.

它改进了动作分布和时序建模，但仍然离不开示范的覆盖范围。没见过的接触状态、抓歪以后怎么恢复、遮挡时怎么估计状态，这些都不是去噪模型能自动解决的；采样步数、chunk horizon 和执行频率也得一起调。

It improves distribution and temporal modeling while remaining dependent on demonstration coverage. Unseen contacts, recovery after bad grasps and hidden-state estimation require additional information or experience. Tune sampling steps, chunk horizon and execution frequency together.

5Flow Matching：共同点与区别Flow matching: common ground and differences

两者学的都是条件生成过程：输入 observation，把噪声变成 action chunk。好处都一样，能表示多峰、高维、有时序结构的动作；部署时的问题也一样，算力有限时多久才能生成一个可用的 chunk。要区分它们，应该比较概率路径、训练的 target 和采样器 [58, 86]。

Both learn a conditional generative process from noise to action chunks. Both can represent multimodal, high-dimensional temporal actions, and both face latency constraints. Distinguish their probability paths, training targets and samplers [58, 86].

Flow Matching 是更一般的路径回归框架，也可以用 diffusion 的概率路径 [58]，所以说“Diffusion 和 Flow 完全对立”并不准确。另外，条件路径是直线，并不意味着学到的边缘向量场会让每个样本都走直线，更不意味着一步 Euler 就够了。到底谁更快，要在相同硬件、相同精度和相同动作时长下比较。

Flow matching can also use diffusion probability paths [58], so the families are not disjoint opposites. Straight conditional interpolation does not guarantee straight sample trajectories under the marginal field, nor sufficient accuracy with one Euler step. Compare speed under matched hardware, accuracy and action horizons.

π0 和 GR00T N1 都把连续动作生成接到了视觉语言特征上 [5, 7]。它们沿用了 action chunk 的思路，但 VLM 条件、混合训练数据和动作专家的结构又带来了新问题。生成机制只是基础模型系统里的一层。

π0 and GR00T N1 condition continuous action generation on vision-language features [5, 7]. They inherit action-chunk ideas while adding new choices in conditioning, data mixtures and expert architecture. The generator is one layer of the foundation-model system.

63D 与等变策略：DP3、EquiBot3D and equivariant policies: DP3 and EquiBot

Diffusion Policy 解决了怎么表示动作分布，但没规定视觉输入必须是 RGB。几何表示和动作生成器是两个独立的选择：完全可以保留 diffusion head，只换掉策略“看世界”的方式。

Diffusion Policy specifies an expressive action generator without requiring RGB observations. Geometry and generation are independent choices: the diffusion head can remain while perception changes.

DP3：稀疏点云条件化的 Diffusion PolicyDP3: diffusion policies conditioned on sparse point clouds

DP3 [102] 用单视角的稀疏点云、一个轻量的点云编码器和机器人状态作为条件，输入动作扩散模型。它的关键不在于把网络做大，而在于让表征直接带上 3D 几何。原作主要用不带颜色的坐标，所以物体颜色变了，输入特征也不会变，这解释了它在外观变化上泛化得好；反过来，如果任务需要区分形状相同、颜色不同的物体，就得另外补语义信息。深度噪声、遮挡、相机外参误差和点云裁剪，部署时照样会有影响。

DP3 [102] conditions an action diffusion model on sparse single-view point clouds, a lightweight point encoder and robot state. Its main change is geometric representation rather than network size. Using uncolored coordinates reduces direct sensitivity to color, but color-defined tasks need additional semantics. Depth noise, occlusion, calibration and cropping remain deployment concerns.

EquiBot：让动作随几何变换一起变化EquiBot: transform actions consistently with geometry

EquiBot [103] 把 SIM(3) 等变结构加进 diffusion policy，让动作分布在平移、旋转和缩放下保持相应的变化。等变不是不变：整个场景转了一个角度，机器人的目标也应该跟着转，而不是输出一模一样的动作。它把一部分泛化能力直接写进了网络结构，不必为每种物体摆放都重新采示范；但几何上的对称，管不到质量、重力、摩擦和工作空间这些限制。

EquiBot [103] builds SIM(3) equivariance into diffusion policies, relating action distributions under translations, rotations and scaling. Equivariance is not invariance: transformed scenes require correspondingly transformed actions. This reduces the need to demonstrate every geometric arrangement, but geometric symmetry does not remove mass, gravity, friction or reachability differences.

7生成式策略的推理效率与闭环时延Generative-policy efficiency and feedback latency

动作生成器必须在控制周期允许的时间内算完。减少去噪步数、蒸馏生成器、并行推理、异步衔接动作块，是不同层面的优化，省下的时间和带来的误差也各不相同。

Action generation must fit a control budget. Fewer denoising steps, generator distillation, parallel decoding and asynchronous chunk execution optimize different components and introduce different errors.

Consistency Policy 与 OneDP：把多步生成蒸馏为少步Consistency Policy and OneDP: distilling multi-step generation

Consistency Policy [104] 从现成的 Diffusion Policy 蒸馏而来：在教师的生成轨迹上建立一致性关系，让学生只调用一次或几次就能预测出动作。OneDP [105] 研究的也是单步生成，把教师扩散策略的表达能力压缩进一个快速生成器。这两种做法和“直接把采样步数调小”不一样：蒸馏额外加了训练目标和学生模型，用来弥补少步采样的误差，计算量并不是白白省下来的。

Consistency Policy [104] distills a pretrained diffusion policy through consistency along its generative trajectories, enabling one or a few calls. OneDP [105] also transfers a diffusion teacher into a fast one-step action generator. Distillation differs from simply reducing the original sampler’s steps: an additional learning objective trains a student to retain useful distributional behavior.

评测时要看的，是同样硬件上的端到端延迟、闭环更新频率和成功率，而不只是网络的吞吐量。还要确认视觉编码的时间算没算进去、batch size 是否一致，以及受到扰动后多久能更新动作。教师数据里没有的恢复行为，蒸馏也变不出来；想让技能从失败中进步，还得引入新的反馈学习机制。

Compare end-to-end latency, feedback rate and success on the same hardware, including vision encoding and batch size. Measure how soon behavior changes after a disturbance. Distillation alone does not supply missing recovery experience; improving skills from failure requires an additional feedback-learning mechanism.

ACT 的 temporal ensembling 融合的是对同一执行时刻的多个预测；RTC 约束的是异步推理时已经承诺要执行的动作；Consistency Policy / OneDP 降低的是生成器本身的计算量。三者可以组合使用，但名称和速度结论不能混着用。

ACT ensembles predictions for the same execution time; RTC handles committed actions during asynchronous inference; Consistency Policy / OneDP reduce generation cost. These mechanisms can compose, but their names and speed claims are not interchangeable.

8UMI：能迁移的观测与动作接口UMI: transferable observation and action interfaces

UMI 让人拿着装了 GoPro 的夹爪，在真实场景里采集示范。它和随便拍的 egocentric 视频不同：夹指的形状、相机的安装位置、连续记录的夹爪开度，加上用 RGB 和 IMU 恢复出的轨迹，一起构成了能直接用于机器人学习的 observation–action 对。原论文的策略实验全部用的是 Diffusion Policy；UMI 是一套采集和部署的接口，不是新的去噪网络 [3]。

UMI records real-world demonstrations with handheld GoPro grippers. Unlike arbitrary egocentric video, matching fingers, camera mounting, continuous opening measurements and RGB/IMU trajectory recovery supply robot-learning observations and actions. Original policy experiments use Diffusion Policy; UMI is an interface framework, not a new denoising network [3].

Relative trajectory 不是逐步的 deltaRelative trajectories are not incremental deltas

同一 chunk 的所有目标都相对本次查询的锚点 。比如未来到前方 2、5、8 cm，relative targets 是 2、5、8；逐步 delta 才是 2、3、3。共同刚体坐标变换会在 中抵消，因此不绑定某个 robot-base frame 或关节数量；但 SLAM 漂移、尺度误差和运动不可达不会因此消失。

Every target in a chunk is relative to the query’s fixed anchor . Targets 2, 5 and 8 cm ahead remain 2, 5 and 8; incremental deltas would be 2, 3 and 3. Common rigid frame transforms cancel in , reducing dependence on a robot base or joint count. Drift, scale errors and infeasible motion do not cancel.

为什么不能说 UMI “没有 embodiment gap”Why “no embodiment gap” is too broad

夹指几何和 wrist view 一致，人的示范就和机器人看到的输入很接近；用相对末端动作，又省掉了关节映射。这是 UMI 的核心优势。但人手拿着走出的轨迹，可能超出机械臂的工作空间、速度或加速度限制；碰撞、奇异位形、负载、柔顺性和夹爪松开的速度也都不同。UMI 需要可行性过滤和 latency matching，正说明剩下的差异还得靠工程去处理。

Matching fingers and wrist views brings demonstrations and robot observations closer; relative EE actions reduce joint correspondence problems. Yet handheld paths can exceed robot reach, speed or acceleration, and differ in collisions, singularities, payload, compliance or release timing. Feasibility filtering and latency matching explicitly address these remaining differences.

腕部相机、机械臂状态和夹爪状态必须对应同一个时刻；发送未来目标时，再按各自的延迟提前发出。千万不要把“旧图像加最新状态”拼成一个现实中从未出现过的 observation。双臂任务还要恢复两个夹爪之间的相对位姿和共同的场景坐标，用局部相对动作，不代表就不用同步和标定了。

Wrist images, arm state and gripper state must refer to a common observation time, with future targets scheduled for each actuator’s delay. Combining old images with the latest state invents an observation that never existed. Bimanual tasks also require inter-gripper pose and shared-frame recovery; local actions do not remove synchronization requirements.

9从桌面双臂到移动操作From tabletop bimanual to mobile manipulation

Mobile ALOHA 把底盘移动和双臂操作放进同一套 Teleop 和学习流程，并用静态 ALOHA 数据和移动任务数据做 co-training [30]。它的意义在于，移动和操作发生在同一条时间线上：底盘停在哪、身体怎么靠过去、两只手怎么配合，本来就是同一个任务里互相牵连的决策。

Mobile ALOHA brings base motion and bimanual manipulation into a common teleoperation and learning setup, co-training mobile-task data with static ALOHA data [30]. Positioning the base, approaching a workspace and coordinating the hands become coupled decisions on one timeline.

一旦能移动，就多出了一堆问题：视野不断变化，需要地图和记忆，底盘定位有误差，长流程中还要能从失败中恢复。轮式底盘和 humanoid 的约束也不同：humanoid 操作时受到的反作用力会影响平衡，手的精度和脚下的支撑必须一起控制。桌面任务上的成功率，不能代表完整家务流程做得好。

Mobility adds changing views, mapping and memory, base-localization errors and long-horizon recovery. Humanoids additionally couple manipulation forces to balance and support contacts. Tabletop success rates do not directly measure a complete household workflow.

10灵巧手、触觉与真机 RLDexterity, touch and real-world RL

两指夹爪靠机械结构限制了接触方式，策略要控制的自由度就少；多指手能做的接触更丰富，但也带来手内换握、打滑和接触难以观测的问题。DexCap 等方案主要解决人手示范和机器人手之间的对应 [4]；DIGIT 这类视觉触觉传感器能提供局部的接触形变信息 [95]。加了传感器，还要考虑标定、时延、耐用性，以及训练数据有没有覆盖这些新输入。

Parallel grippers constrain contact mechanically; multi-finger hands enable richer contacts but add regrasping, slip and observability challenges. DexCap addresses demonstration correspondence [4], while optical tactile sensors such as DIGIT measure local contact deformation [95]. Sensor additions still require calibration, timing, durability and training coverage.

HIL-SERL：根据真实执行结果改进HIL-SERL: improve using real outcomes

HIL-SERL 把示范 buffer、在线 RL、成功分类器和人工接管组合成一个系统 [60]。示范帮策略先走到有意义的状态，人工接管负责纠错、省掉无效的探索，RL 再根据结果继续改进。它和 DAgger 的区别在于，学习信号不只有专家动作，还有 reward 和 critic 的更新。

HIL-SERL combines a demonstration buffer, online RL, a success classifier and human intervention [60]. Demonstrations seed useful states, interventions correct behavior, and RL improves from outcomes. Unlike pure DAgger, its learning signal includes reward and critic updates as well as expert actions.

从 ACT 或 diffusion policy 转向 RL，要回答几个问题：奖励怎么定义，生成出来的动作价值怎么评估，在哪里探索，失败了怎么恢复。π*0.6 / RECAP 和 RLT 是大模型 Post-training 方向上的相关工作 [11, 49]；不能因为最终策略都用 flow matching，就以为它们的 Post-training 算法也一样。

Moving from ACT or diffusion imitation to RL requires rewards, action valuation, an exploration mechanism and recovery. π*0.6/RECAP and RLT address related foundation-model post-training questions [11, 49]. A shared flow-matching policy head does not imply an identical RL algorithm.

11从模仿到反馈学习：Diffusion-QL 与 DPPOFrom imitation to feedback: Diffusion-QL and DPPO

Diffusion-QL：在离线数据附近优化动作价值Diffusion-QL: value optimization near offline data

Diffusion-QL [106] 把“用扩散模型拟合数据里的行为”和“最大化 Q 值”结合起来：既保留数据中多峰的动作，又借 critic 偏向价值高的动作。它是一个 offline RL 方法，证据主要来自 D4RL 等离线基准；不能因为也用了 diffusion，就把它当作原版 Diffusion Policy 的真机验证。它最大的风险仍是 critic 会高估数据之外的动作。

Diffusion-QL [106] combines diffusion-based behavior fitting with action-value maximization, preserving multimodal behavior while favoring higher-value actions. It is an offline-RL method evaluated on offline-control benchmarks such as D4RL, not the real-robot evidence for the original Diffusion Policy. Out-of-distribution value error remains central.

DPPO：把去噪过程作为策略优化的一部分DPPO: optimize the denoising process as part of the policy

DPPO [107] 把环境中的决策和模型内部的去噪过程看成两层 MDP，在每个去噪步骤上（其转移是可以算出概率的高斯分布）做 policy gradient / PPO 更新，这样就不用去求最终动作边缘分布的 log-probability 了。预训练的示范给出初始行为，在线奖励再引导它学会恢复、执行得更稳。它的 sim-to-real 装配实验和 SGS 的 RGB 装配实验设置不同，观测、reset 和奖励要分开比较。

DPPO [107] treats environment decisions and internal denoising as a two-layer MDP. Gaussian denoising transitions support policy-gradient/PPO updates without requiring a closed-form marginal likelihood for the final action. Demonstrations initialize behavior and online rewards improve execution and recovery. Its sim-to-real assembly protocol differs from SGS’s RGB setup; compare observations, resets and rewards separately.

动作表示、优化算法、训练时的状态分布，是三个独立的维度：Flow / diffusion 决定行为怎么表示，PPO / Q-learning 等决定行为怎么改进，SGS 决定在哪些状态下练。一个新系统可以三者同时改，但消融实验应该分别验证。

Action representation, optimization and training-state distribution are separate axes. Flow/diffusion represent behavior, PPO/Q-learning improve it, and SGS chooses where to practice. Systems and ablations should distinguish these changes.

12SGS：在仿真里学装配SGS: learning assembly skills in simulation

SGS（Success-Guided Sampling）关心的不是动作模型，而是训练时在哪些状态下练。论文 A Balanced Data Diet [55] 根据成功率来决定采样，让大规模 PPO 学装配和运动技能的效率更高。本节只看操作部分：ACT / Diffusion Policy 靠专家示范，HIL-SERL 靠真机交互改进，SGS 的操作实验则在仿真里获得交互反馈，再把视觉策略迁移到真机上。

Success-Guided Sampling (SGS) shifts attention from the action model to the training distribution. A Balanced Data Diet [55] uses success-guided sampling to improve large-scale PPO for assembly and locomotion. This section focuses on manipulation: unlike demonstration-based ACT / Diffusion Policy or real-interaction learning in HIL-SERL, the SGS assembly experiments learn from simulated interaction and transfer a visual policy to hardware.

为什么更多并行环境还不够Why more parallel environments are insufficient

如果初始状态均匀随机抽，大量算力会浪费在已经学会的配置，或者暂时根本不可能成功的配置上。SGS 记录每种配置最近的成功率，让练习集中在“刚好够得着”的地方，同时对所有配置都保留少量采样。它改的是训练分布，并没有替换 PPO；论文也明确说，这是在自动课程学习这条线上的改进，不是新发明了课程学习 [55]。

Uniform resets can waste computation on configurations already mastered or currently impossible. SGS tracks recent success per configuration and emphasizes the policy’s capability frontier while retaining nonzero sampling everywhere. It changes the training distribution, not PPO; the paper explicitly positions it within automatic curriculum learning [55].

SGS 为每个重置配置估计近期成功率 ，再计算采样权重。目标成功率 决定优先练习的位置， 控制集中程度；随后对 做带温度的 softmax，得到采样概率 [55]。

SGS estimates recent success for each reset configuration. Target success sets the preferred difficulty and controls concentration; a temperature-scaled softmax over gives sampling probabilities [55].

从仿真策略到 RGB 真机策略From simulated teachers to RGB hardware policies

装配训练用了 reaching、stable grasp、near-goal 等几类程序化生成的重置状态，同一个任务内共享奖励。迁移到真机时，先在仿真里训练一个看点云的 teacher，再用 DAgger 蒸馏成看侧视和腕部 RGB 的 student；部署到 UR5e 上时，没有做任何真机训练或微调 [55]。所以“纯仿真”说的是学习数据的来源，并不是说这个系统不需要物理建模、相机配置和控制接口方面的工程。

Assembly training uses programmatic reaching, stable-grasp and near-goal resets with a shared reward within the domain. For hardware transfer, a point-cloud teacher is distilled using DAgger into a side/wrist RGB student, all in simulation, then deployed to UR5e without real-world policy training or fine-tuning [55]. Simulation-only learning still requires physical modeling, cameras and control engineering.

结果：三种装配任务的迁移差距各不相同Results: transfer differs across assembly tasks

真机上第一次就插入成功的比例分别是 12.5%、28.57% 和 76%，允许重试后会更高 [55]。齿轮啮合的结果很好，螺母装配的差距仍然明显。Demo 里值得留意的是策略的恢复动作，以及借桌面把物体重新摆正的行为；不能拿最好的那个数字代表所有任务。

First-insertion success is 12.5%, 28.57% and 76%, respectively, below success allowing retries [55]. Gear meshing transfers strongly; nut assembly retains a substantial gap. Inspect recovery and object reorientation against the table rather than summarizing all tasks with the best number.

X thread 与技术局限The X thread and technical boundaries

Octi Zhang 在 2026 年 10 月 9 日发了原始的 X thread [56]，介绍不用示范就学会的装配和运动结果。看宣传视频时要分清真机和仿真：论文的真机验证只有 UR5e 上的三种装配任务，并不是所有展示的任务都在硬件上验证过。

Octi Zhang’s original X thread, dated October 9, 2026 [56], introduces the demonstration-free results. Distinguish simulation from hardware: the paper’s real-world evaluation covers three UR5e assembly tasks, not every showcased task.

最大的限制来自重置集合：消融实验里去掉 near-goal 重置后，插杆任务就学不会了。仿真里的接触参数也要调，真机的板面还贴了胶带，减小反光和摩擦上的差异 [55]。所以“不需要示范”不等于没有先验：reset、reward、物理模型和控制接口一起决定了能学到什么。Agentic Real2Sim 可以帮忙搭训练环境；SGS 研究的则是在给定的环境里该多练什么。

Reset coverage is crucial: removing near-goal resets prevents learning in the reported rod-in-hole ablation. Contact settings were adjusted and the hardware board was taped to reduce reflection and friction mismatch [55]. Demonstration-free does not mean prior-free: resets, rewards, physics and control interfaces define the learning problem. Agentic Real2Sim can help construct environments; SGS allocates practice within a given environment.
