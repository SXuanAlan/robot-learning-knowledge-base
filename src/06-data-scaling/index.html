1数据为什么有用：闭环与信息Why data helps: feedback and information

机器人需要学会的，是在不断变化的世界里，根据当前观测选择动作，并利用后续反馈继续完成目标。数据的价值因此取决于它减少了哪一种决策不确定性：物体在哪里、该做什么、动作能否执行、接触会怎样，以及偏离计划后如何回来。采集设备只是获得这些信息的途径。

A robot must choose actions from observations, then use feedback to keep moving toward a goal. Data is useful when it reduces uncertainty about the scene, the next action, execution, contact or recovery. The recording device is one way to obtain that information.

Shuran Song 在 CoRL 2023 提出数据要同时具备 scalable、reusable、robot-complete 三种性质 [35]。沿这个问题展开，本文用四个维度读数据：信息是否充分、动作是否对齐、场景是否覆盖、获得有效样本的成本有多高。这是分析框架，不是对各种采集方式的固定排名。

Shuran Song’s CoRL 2023 keynote asks for scalable, reusable and robot-complete data [35]. Building on that question, this article examines four dimensions: information sufficiency, action alignment, coverage, and the cost of useful samples. These are analytical dimensions, not fixed rankings of recording methods.

UMI 是不是没有 embodiment gap？Does UMI eliminate the embodiment gap?

1 · 身体与动作语义1 · Body and action semantics人手的关节动作如何对应机械臂与夹爪？UMI 用同构末端接口和 EE trajectory，绕过许多关节空间映射问题。How do human joints correspond to robot actions? UMI’s matched gripper interface and EE trajectories avoid much joint-space correspondence.
2 · 能否及时执行2 · Executability and timing同一个末端目标，机器人能否够到、承载并及时跟上？关节极限、速度、柔顺性和时延仍由具体机器人决定。Can the robot reach, load and track the target on time? Joint limits, speed, compliance and delay remain robot-dependent.
3 · 看见什么3 · What is observed相机相对夹爪的一致安装缩小视角差异；遮挡、曝光、材质和场景分布仍会变化。Matching camera-to-gripper mounting reduces viewpoint shift; occlusion, exposure, materials and scenes still vary.

更准确的说法是：UMI 在匹配的末端接口下显著缩小 embodiment gap，而不是消除所有跨本体迁移问题。如果只把 gap 定义为“手的形状与动作标签如何对应夹爪”，差异可以很小；若还包含可达性、动力学和接触能力，就不能说为零。本表为便于诊断，将后者单列为 Execution / S0；这种划分是阅读框架，三层在物理上仍会相互影响。

UMI substantially reduces embodiment mismatch under a matched end-effector interface; it does not eliminate all cross-body transfer problems. A narrow definition focused on gripper geometry and action correspondence can yield a small gap. A broader definition including reach, dynamics and contact capability cannot be called zero. This table separates the latter as Execution / S0 for diagnosis, while recognizing that the layers interact.

例如人手拿着 UMI 可以绕过障碍物移动，但目标机械臂可能在途中碰撞或进入奇异位形；相同释放时刻的投掷，换一个夹爪时延也会改变结果。采到真实世界视频避免了模拟液体、布料的成本，却不自动提供反事实结果、失败恢复或力监督。“无需仿真建模”因此比“无问题”更准确。

A handheld UMI trajectory may pass through poses where the target arm collides or becomes singular. A different gripper delay can change a throw despite identical nominal release times. Recording real cloth or liquid avoids simulation modeling, but does not automatically supply counterfactual outcomes, recovery data or force labels. “No simulator needed” is therefore more precise than “no issue.”

2Teleop：动作可执行，覆盖仍有限Teleop: executable actions, incomplete coverage

Teleop 的关键优势，是把示范动作放在目标机器人的可执行空间里。观测、控制命令和后果来自同一个物理系统，因而减少了人到机器人的动作对应问题。ALOHA 用低成本主从机械臂收集双臂示范，ACT 从这些序列学习执行 [2]；下面把“人控制时”与“模型执行时”并排放，区分数据采集和策略能力。

Teleoperation places demonstrations in the target robot’s executable action space. Observations, commands and outcomes come from the same physical system, reducing human-to-robot action ambiguity. ALOHA collects bimanual demonstrations with leader–follower arms; ACT learns execution from those sequences [2]. Collection and autonomous rollout are shown separately below.

但“在真机上采集”不能推出“没有迁移问题”。相机位置、机械臂、控制频率或场景改变都会产生分布差异；只录成功轨迹，也可能没有机器人自己犯错后的状态。示范者按熟练路径前进，部署策略却会进入另一组状态，这就是为什么增加同一路径的小时数未必能补上失败恢复。

Real-robot collection does not eliminate transfer problems. Camera placement, hardware, control rates and environments can change. Successful demonstrations may also omit states caused by the policy’s own mistakes: additional hours along the expert’s path need not teach recovery from the learner’s path.

一个容易被忽略的细节是时间：操作者发出的命令、控制器接收的目标、机器人实际到达的位置并不是同一个量。把延迟后的状态误当成当下动作，会让策略学到错位的对应关系。采集成本也应计算校准、重置与失败清理，而不只计算操作员按下录制按钮的时间。

Timing matters too: an operator’s command, a controller’s target and the robot’s achieved position are different quantities. Misaligned timestamps corrupt the observation–action relationship. Collection cost includes calibration, resets and cleanup, not just the time spent recording.

3UMI 与 Wearable：设计可迁移的接口UMI and wearables: designing transfer

UMI 把成本从“带着机器人到处示范”转移到“把人的轨迹变成机器人可执行的目标”。手持夹爪在真实环境里接触物体，记录视觉和末端运动；相对轨迹、相机配置与 Latency Matching 共同支撑迁移 [3]。这不是天然消除了动力学差异，而是主动设计一个更容易对齐的接口。

UMI shifts cost from bringing robots to every demonstration to making human trajectories executable by robots. Its hand-held grippers record real interactions; relative trajectories, camera design and latency matching support transfer [3]. The interface is engineered to reduce mismatch rather than assuming identical dynamics.

这里最有解释力的比较，是“哪些信息在接口中保留下来了”。Specialist 让手持端与机器人端共享手指和相机位置，但作者说明它不记录力 [26]。Reward 的 Omnibody Hand 则增加触觉等信号 [22]。两者都可以扩大人的采集范围，却给模型提供了不同的接触信息；用一个“Wearable”标签不足以判断它们能教会什么。

The useful distinction is what survives the interface. Specialist shares finger geometry and camera placement across collection and execution, but does not record force [26]. Reward’s Omnibody Hand adds tactile information [22]. Both scale human collection, yet expose different contact signals; the label “wearable” does not describe what each can teach.

例如，把杯子放到桌上，视觉与末端轨迹常常已经有用；拧紧接头时，相似的手部轨迹可能对应不同扭矩和摩擦。缺失的量可以由额外传感器、反馈控制或交互经验补足，但不会因为视频更多而自动变成可观测。

For placing a cup, vision and end-effector motion may provide useful supervision. For tightening a fitting, similar motion can involve different torque and friction. Additional sensing, feedback control or interaction can resolve missing information; more video alone does not make every hidden variable observable.

4Egocentric：看见行为，如何学到动作Egocentric: from observing behavior to acting

Egocentric 数据首先擅长覆盖人实际生活里的任务与场景。它可能只有 RGB，也可能带头部和手部位姿。Ego4D、EgoDex 和 EgoMimic 的数据与训练目标各不相同 [27, 28, 29]。读这类工作，应先分清“看到了任务过程”“恢复了人的运动”与“得到了机器人的动作监督”这三个层次。

Egocentric data can cover tasks and environments from everyday human activity. It may contain RGB alone or include head and hand poses. Ego4D, EgoDex and EgoMimic differ in data and learning objectives [27, 28, 29]. Observing a task, reconstructing human motion and supervising robot actions are distinct achievements.

EgoMimic 将人和机器人的数据对齐并联合训练 [28]。这个例子说明：人的数据可以扩大场景与行为覆盖，机器人的数据可以提供动作语义的锚点。PI 的 human-to-robot 研究也报告，随着机器人预训练数据增加，人类视频带来的迁移能力增强 [36]。因此，Human Data 与 Robot Data 可以互补，不能只按小时数互相替换。

EgoMimic aligns human and robot data for joint training [28]. Human data can broaden coverage while robot data anchors action meaning. PI also reports stronger human-video transfer as its robot pretraining data grows [36]. Human and robot data can therefore complement each other rather than substitute hour for hour.

从信息上说，单段视频只能告诉我们发生过什么，通常不能唯一确定“如果换一个动作会怎样”。学习世界模型、逆动力学或动作潜变量可以缩小这个缺口，但模型的推断不等于实测动作或接触力。必须由目标机器人的闭环结果检验这种推断是否足够。

A video records what happened; it generally does not uniquely identify what would have happened under another action. World models, inverse dynamics and latent actions can help bridge that gap, but inferred actions are not measured actions or forces. Target-robot execution must test whether the inference is sufficient.

5Simulation：干预能力与模型偏差Simulation: intervention and model bias

仿真的独特价值是可干预：能够重置状态、改变动作、重复失败，并观察后果。对 RL 而言，它提供的不只是更多轨迹，而是一个可反复查询的交互过程。代价是模型偏差：如果接触、摩擦或变形不对，策略可能学会利用模拟器的错误。

Simulation’s distinctive value is intervention: reset a state, vary an action, repeat failures and observe outcomes. For RL, this is a queryable interaction process, not merely a larger recording. Its cost is model bias: policies can exploit incorrect contact, friction or deformation.

在全身控制里，明确的运动目标与可计算的奖励让大规模仿真训练具有吸引力，SONIC 与 Helix 02 的低层控制提供了例子 [10, 13]。精细操作的难点则常在环境与接触模型。Sim Teleop 用人来提供行为，但并不修复物理模型；它同时继承人的采集吞吐量和仿真偏差。

For whole-body control, explicit motion targets and computable rewards make large-scale simulation attractive, as illustrated by SONIC and Helix 02’s low-level control [10, 13]. Fine manipulation often stresses environment and contact models. Human teleoperation in simulation supplies behavior without fixing the underlying physics.

据此，真实数据与仿真可以分工：真实交互校准模型与目标分布，仿真拓展动作探索和扰动，真机评测检查残余偏差。Agentic Real2Sim 试图降低建场景的成本，但场景搭建得更快与动力学更准确是两件需要分别验证的事 [24]。

Real interaction can calibrate models and deployment conditions; simulation can expand action exploration and perturbations; hardware evaluation measures remaining mismatch. Agentic Real2Sim targets scene-building cost [24], while physical accuracy remains a separate empirical question.

6数据进入学习的哪一层Where data enters learning

同一份数据可以在不同层起作用。只有图像的记录可以训练表示；带时间顺序的视频可以训练预测；对齐的动作可以监督策略；结果、偏好和奖励可以评价并改进决策。训练配方应按“这份数据约束了哪个变量”描述，而不是把所有来源混成一个总小时数。

One dataset can serve different layers: images train representations, sequences train prediction, aligned actions supervise policies, and outcomes, preferences or rewards guide improvement. A training recipe should state what each source constrains rather than collapse everything into total hours.

一个合理的数据策略，是先定位部署失败集中在哪一项，再决定采什么。语义没懂、位姿没对齐、接触失控和长任务累积误差，需要的增量数据并不相同。这也把数据设计与 Imitation Learning、Offline RL 和 Online RL 接到同一个问题上。

A useful collection strategy starts with the failure mode. Misunderstood intent, pose mismatch, unstable contact and accumulated long-task errors call for different additional data. This connects data design to imitation learning, offline RL and online RL.

7Scaling：有效信息，而不只是小时数Scaling useful information, beyond hours

“数据扩大十倍”至少可能指十倍帧数、十倍独立示范，或十倍任务与环境。相邻帧高度相关，重复示范也不一定覆盖新的失败状态。本文把 Scaling 的核心问题写成：在固定部署目标和总成本下，增加什么数据，能让闭环成功率提高多少？

“Ten times more data” might mean more frames, independent demonstrations, or tasks and environments. Adjacent frames are correlated; repeated demonstrations need not cover new failures. The central question is: for a fixed deployment objective and total cost, which additional data improves closed-loop success, and by how much?

Generalist、Skild 和 Dyna 分别讨论交互数据、In-Context Learning 和 WAM 的扩展规律 [9, 19, 18]。它们是各自实验设置下的证据。横向比较时，至少要固定任务与硬件、训练与测试划分、模型与算力，以及适配新任务所用的额外数据；否则很难把收益归因于数据来源。

Generalist, Skild and Dyna study scaling in different interaction-data, in-context-learning and WAM settings [9, 19, 18]. Each provides evidence within its own setup. Attribution requires controlling tasks, hardware, train/test splits, model capacity, compute and extra task-adaptation data.

最后回到第一节：采集速度决定“能拿到多少”，信息与动作接口决定“能用来学什么”，覆盖范围和闭环评测决定“是否真的有用”。把这些变量拆开后，Teleop、UMI、Egocentric 和 Simulation 更像互补的实验工具，而不是只能押注其中之一的阵营。

Collection throughput determines how much can be gathered; information and action interfaces determine what it teaches; coverage and closed-loop evaluation determine whether it helps. Teleoperation, UMI, egocentric recording and simulation are often complementary experimental tools.
