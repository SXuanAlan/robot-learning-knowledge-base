NVIDIA

NVIDIA 同时研究基础策略、世界动作模型、仿真训练和全身控制。理解它的路线需要区分 GR00T、DreamZero、SONIC 各自负责哪一层，以及新发布的 GR00T 2 如何把这些方向接起来。

NVIDIA works on foundation policies, world-action models, simulated learning and whole-body control. Separate the roles of GR00T, DreamZero and SONIC, then examine how GR00T 2 connects them.

不能把 NVIDIA 简化成“靠仿真数据的 VLA 公司”。这些工作处在不同层：有的学习语义到动作，有的提供动态先验，有的承担机器人稳定执行。仿真可以参与训练、数据生成或评测，三种角色的证据不能互相替代。

NVIDIA is not adequately described by one model or data label. These projects address semantics-to-action, dynamics priors and stable execution. Simulation can support training, data generation or evaluation; evidence for one role does not establish another.

2从动作模型到世界动作模型From action models to world-action models

DreamZero 把未来视频和动作的联合建模用于机器人策略 [14]。GR00T 2 则明确采用联合去噪未来视频与动作的 diffusion transformer，并用本体对应的输出模块落到机器人命令 [50]。视频预测增加了动作以外的监督，但也增加了计算和生成误差。

DreamZero studies joint future-video/action modeling for robot policies [14]. GR00T 2 uses a diffusion transformer to denoise both and body-specific outputs to produce commands [50]. Video adds supervision, computation and another source of prediction error.

读这类模型时，最重要的是区分三个视频：模型当前看到的真实历史、它预测的未来、机器人真正执行后的结果。预测未来可以帮助学习，却不是已经发生的真实动作。生成很漂亮而闭环不成功，正是需要被实验识别的失败模式。

Distinguish observed history, predicted future and actual execution. A generated future may support learning, but it is not a physical result. Visually plausible predictions with failed execution are a failure mode experiments must expose.

3GR00T 2：跨数据适应的边界GR00T 2: interpreting cross-adaptation

GR00T 2 报告将人类 Egocentric、UMI 与机器人 Teleop 混合预训练，并展示从人、手持夹爪和可穿戴手向不同机器人迁移的 X-Adapt 设置 [50]。某任务没有目标机器人示范，不等于该机器人完全没有数据；任务无关 play 可以提供本体对应关系。

GR00T 2 mixes human egocentric, UMI and robot teleoperation data, with X-Adapt examples transferring across capture interfaces and bodies [50]. No target-robot demonstration of a task does not mean no data from that robot: task-agnostic play can ground its embodiment.

与 Reward 的共享硬件接口相比，这里更突出混合数据与模型中的跨本体对应。两个方向并不矛盾：更好的采集接口可以让模型少学一部分差异，而更强的模型可以让接口不必完全一致。

Compared with Reward’s shared hardware, the emphasis is mixed-data and model-based correspondence. Better capture can remove mismatch, while better models can tolerate interfaces that differ.

4Turbo 与 SONIC：怎样让 WAM 跑在机器人上Turbo and SONIC: bringing WAMs onboard

Turbo 使用更小的联合模型与更紧凑的视频表示，G1 示例通过 SONIC 执行全身运动。报告把上层模型预测与控制频率分开：Thor 上预测约 124 ms，运动块约每秒规划一次，由 SONIC 以 50 Hz 执行 [50]。这些数字对应不同环节，不能统称“模型控制频率”。

Turbo reduces joint-model size and video representation cost; its G1 examples use SONIC for whole-body execution. The report distinguishes approximately 124 ms prediction on Thor, roughly once-per-second chunk planning, and 50 Hz SONIC execution [50]. These measure different stages.

这里的结构与 Figure、Dyna 有共通之处：上层模型提供目标，下层处理更快的动态。值得比较的不是谁用了同样的 S0 名称，而是动作接口、观测更新、扰动恢复和计算预算。

This resembles Figure and Dyna: upper layers provide goals and lower layers handle faster dynamics. Compare interfaces, observation updates, recovery and compute, not shared layer names.

5仿真路线与 SGS 的连接Where SGS fits into the simulation route

SGS 在已有任务配置中按成功率分配练习，研究大规模 PPO 的探索瓶颈 [55]。它与 WAM 提供的是不同能力：一个改训练分布，一个改模型和监督。前者的真机装配结果不能自动变成 GR00T 的能力，后者的多任务预训练也不能替代接触建模。详见 Manipulation Policies & Skills 的 SGS 专节。

SGS allocates practice among task configurations by success rate to address large-scale PPO exploration [55]. It changes the training distribution; WAMs change models and supervision. Neither result should be attributed to the other. See the SGS section.

阅读顺序可以是 GR00T N1 → DreamZero → GR00T 2，控制部分补 SONIC。不同报告开放的代码、数据和权重范围不同，项目页上的发布计划也不等于资源已经可下载。

Read GR00T N1 → DreamZero → GR00T 2, with SONIC for control. Reports differ in available code, data and weights; announced release plans do not imply resources are already downloadable.
