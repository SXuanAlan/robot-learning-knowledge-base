Skild AI

Skild S1 将视频示范作为新任务的输入，强调固定模型权重下的上下文学习。后续 Physical Self-Play 则研究如何在预训练之后，用仿真中的竞争继续改善动态技能。

Skild S1 uses demonstration videos to specify tasks under fixed weights. Physical Self-Play explores a later stage: improving dynamic skills through simulated competition after pretraining.

1S1：示范是提示，还是训练集S1: demonstrations as prompts or training data

S1 发布材料将视频示范作为任务指定方式，并强调不为每个新任务微调权重；讨论中特别区分未见任务、任务组合和长时序行为 [19]。这是对基础模型价值的一种定义：预训练不只让后训练更省数据，还应让一些任务能直接通过示范指定。

S1 presents video demonstrations as task prompts without per-task weight updates, emphasizing novelty, composition and horizon [19]. Its definition of foundation-model value goes beyond cheaper fine-tuning toward direct task specification through demonstrations.

不过，看到一段示范后成功并不自动说明学会了新技能：它可能选择了已有技能、复制了动作顺序，或学到了新的接触方式。要区分这些情况，需要控制训练覆盖、改变物体和布局，并用相同动作元素构造不同任务。

Success after a demonstration can mean selecting a known skill, adopting an order, or learning a new contact strategy. Separate these with controlled training coverage, changed layouts and tasks assembled from shared action elements.

2公开机制与证据边界Disclosed mechanisms and evidence limits

S1 首篇报告聚焦 ICL 能力，明确把详细训练方式留给后续文章 [19]。因此不能从 Demo 反推它一定采用某种具体 transformer、diffusion 或视频预测结构。Self-Play 材料把人类视频、手套、仿真与 Teleop 列为更大体系的数据来源 [52]；这也说明公司路线不等于单一数据范式。

The S1 article focuses on ICL and leaves detailed training to later reports [19]. Its demos do not identify a specific transformer, diffusion or video-prediction architecture. The Self-Play overview lists human videos, gloves, simulation and teleoperation in the broader system [52].

从机制上可以提出可检验的问题：模型怎样在提示里识别任务意图，怎样把人的动作对应到机器人，怎样知道示范中的哪一步已经完成？这些问题分别涉及表征、对应关系与记忆；“one-shot”这个词本身没有回答它们。

Mechanistic questions remain: how does the model infer intent, align demonstrated and robot actions, and identify completed steps? These concern representations, correspondence and memory; “one-shot” alone does not answer them.

3Physical Self-Play：把试错搬到仿真Physical Self-Play: simulated improvement

Physical Self-Play 展示在仿真中让策略与自身竞争，研究动态足球行为的后训练 [52]。竞争能持续改变挑战难度，但“对手越来越强”和“机器人越来越通用”是不同主张：它首先改善的是所定义环境和奖励中的能力。

Physical Self-Play studies simulated competition for dynamic soccer post-training [52]. Opponents can keep the challenge evolving, but improvement within a specified game and reward does not establish general robot competence.

与 SGS 这样的成功率采样相比，两者都调整学习中遇到的经验，但机制不同：self-play 通过对手塑造分布，SGS 根据已有配置的成功率分配练习。仿真是否包含真实接触、执行器限制和可观测信息，决定这些能力能否带回机器人。

Like success-guided sampling, self-play changes the experiences encountered during learning. It does so through opponents rather than sampling a fixed configuration set by success rates. Transfer still depends on contacts, actuation and observations being represented adequately.
