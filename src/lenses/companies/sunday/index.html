Robot Learning KB / Physical AI / Sunday

Sunday

Sunday 把数据问题和机器人 Memo 的硬件设计放在一起解决：先让人的操作能直接转成机器人能执行的示范，再研究少量 Post-training 带来的改进能不能用到没去过的家庭。ACT-1 和 ACT-2 关注的重点不一样。

Sunday links its data strategy to Memo’s hardware: make human demonstrations executable by the robot, then test whether small post-training improvements transfer to unseen homes. ACT-1 and ACT-2 emphasize different parts of that argument.

1ACT、Diffusion Policy 与 UMI 在这里汇合Where ACT, Diffusion Policy and UMI meet

Tony Zhao 的 ALOHA / ACT 研究的是低成本双臂采集和 action chunk 学习；Cheng Chi 的 Diffusion Policy 和 UMI 研究的是多峰动作分布，以及不需要机器人在场的示范采集 [2, 1, 3, 31, 32]。所以 Sunday 的思路不难理解：把“学什么样的策略”和“怎样拿到能迁移的数据”放进同一个产品里解决。

Tony Zhao’s ALOHA / ACT studies low-cost bimanual demonstrations and action chunks; Cheng Chi’s Diffusion Policy and UMI study action distributions and capture away from robots [2, 1, 3, 31, 32]. Sunday brings policy learning and transferable data collection into one product.

不过，产品叫 ACT-1、ACT-2，并不说明它们沿用了早期 ACT 的网络结构。研究上的渊源只是背景，现在的架构要以公开的技术材料为准。本文也不会从师生关系去推断公司之间有合作，或谁是谁的顾问。

The product names ACT-1 and ACT-2 do not establish that they reuse the original ACT architecture. Research lineage explains context; current implementations require current technical evidence. Advising relationships do not establish corporate affiliations.

2ACT-1：在硬件上解决人与机器人的对应ACT-1: embodying the correspondence

ACT-1 由三部分组成：Skill Capture Glove、机器人 Memo 和 Skill Transform。Skill Transform 负责对齐视觉和运动学观测，把手套采到的人的动作转换成机器人格式的数据；官方特别强调，ACT-1 没有用任何 Teleop 轨迹 [12]。这条路线是主动去缩小人和机器人的差异，而不是一味堆未经处理的视频。

ACT-1 combines the Skill Capture Glove, Memo and Skill Transform, which aligns visual and kinematic observations into robot-form data. The release states that ACT-1 uses no teleoperation trajectories [12]. The route deliberately reduces human–robot mismatch, beyond simply collecting more video.

硬件和数据一起设计，公司就能自己掌控相机视角、抓取几何和动作空间，但数据的价值也就绑定在这一款机器人上了。和 Reward 主打的多本体相比，对 Sunday 更值得追问的是：只用一种机器人，在一个家庭里学会的纠错行为，能不能低成本地推广到别的家庭？

Co-design gives control over camera views, grasp geometry and action spaces, while making data value depend on embodiment consistency. Compared with Reward’s cross-body emphasis, ask whether a correction learned on Memo transfers cheaply across homes.

3ACT-2：Pretraining 怎样降低纠错成本ACT-2: pretraining for transferable correction

ACT-2 Preview 的核心实验是：Post-training 流程不变，只加大 Pretraining 的规模，看训练过的场景和没见过的场景之间的差距会不会缩小。报告还用四段不同的示范，各自 SFT 出一个模型，学会了四种不同的叠衣服方式 [43]。注意这里的“一次示范”是更新了权重的，和 Generalist、Skild 那种不改权重的 ICL 要分开。

ACT-2 Preview scales pretraining under a common post-training procedure and studies the gap between familiar and held-out settings. It also fine-tunes four model copies with one demonstration each to teach different folding techniques [43]. That updates weights, unlike fixed-weight ICL claims.

它在叠衣服并叠放这个任务上成功了 778/785 次，约 99.1%，覆盖九类衣物 [43]。这个数字有明确的分母，但它是单个重复任务的成功率，不是完整家务流程的成功率。出了小错能不能自己恢复、需不需要人重新摆一下、一整天能服务多久，是系统层面的另一组问题。

The folding-and-stacking evaluation reports 778/785 successes, about 99.1%, across nine garment types [43]. This supplies a repeated-operation denominator, not a full household-workflow success rate. Recoverability, repositioning and day-long service time remain separate questions.

4和 Figure 的争论：背后的技术问题Read the Figure debate as a technical question

Tony 对 Figure 的两次公开评论，一次是关于玻璃和塑料对接触的不同要求，一次是关于在新场景里的表现够不够可靠 [37, 41]。材质不同，摔坏的代价、摩擦和容错空间都不同；泛化和可靠性则要靠明确的任务定义和反复试验来验证。公开的争论有助于把问题摆出来，但一家公司对另一家的批评，不能代替第三方评测。

Tony’s public comments on Figure concern material-dependent contact and reliability in new scenes [37, 41]. Materials change failure cost and tolerance; generalization and reliability need task definitions and repeated trials. Public disagreement identifies questions, not an independent evaluation.

ACT-2 测的是叠一件衣服，Helix 2.5 测的是一整项家务，分母不同，99.1% 和 56.4% 不能直接比。横向综述里保留了两轮原帖、Figure 的回应视频和各任务的统计。对 Sunday 来说，关键要看这种可靠性的提升，能不能推广到更多种类的接触和任务组合上。

ACT-2 garment folding and Helix 2.5 household behaviors use different success units, so 99.1% and 56.4% cannot be ranked directly. The survey preserves both exchanges and task-level counts. Sunday’s next test is whether reliability improvements extend across contact types and task combinations.
