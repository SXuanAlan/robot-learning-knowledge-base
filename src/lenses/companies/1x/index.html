1X

1X 的 World Model 有两种不同角色：先在虚拟未来里评估策略，再用生成视频作为行动中间表示。理解这两个方向，才能判断一段漂亮的预测视频离真实自主操作还有多远。

1X uses world models in two distinct roles: evaluating policies through predicted futures, and generating video as an intermediate representation for action. Separating them clarifies what a convincing video says about physical autonomy.

12025：用世界模型评估策略2025: evaluating policies with a world model

早期 1X World Model 输入历史与候选动作，预测未来视觉和结果，用于比较策略与 checkpoint [54]。它的价值是降低真机评测成本，让同一初始条件下的反事实比较更容易；它并没有消除世界模型本身的评测需求。

The earlier 1X World Model predicts visual futures and outcomes conditioned on histories and candidate actions, supporting policy and checkpoint comparison [54]. It can reduce hardware evaluation cost and enable controlled counterfactuals, but the evaluator itself still needs validation.

关键测试是模型能否保持策略排序，并识别真实失败。若一个策略恰好利用了世界模型的误差，在模型里越优化反而可能越偏离现实。因而需要保留没有用于拟合的真机结果，持续检查仿真预测与真实排序的相关性。

The test is whether it preserves policy rankings and recognizes failures. Optimizing against model errors can improve predicted outcomes while worsening reality. Held-out physical results are needed to audit agreement with the learned evaluator.

22026：先生成视频，再提取动作2026: generate video, then infer actions

1XWM 的策略路线从文字与起始图像生成未来视频，再由 inverse dynamics model（IDM）提取动作；报告把视频训练与动作落地分为两个阶段 [53]。这与直接输出动作的 VLA，以及联合生成视频和动作的 WAM，是不同的因子分解。

The 1XWM policy generates future video from text and an initial frame, then uses an inverse dynamics model (IDM) to recover actions [53]. This differs from direct VLA action prediction and joint video–action generation.

3动作落地的数据成本没有消失The data cost of action grounding remains

报告中的 IDM 使用约四百小时机器人数据，包含无任务的 play；视频模型则利用人类 Egocentric 与 NEO 数据进行对齐 [53]。所以“某个任务没有机器人示范”和“系统没有用过机器人数据”是不同条件。IDM 需要知道这具身体如何移动，才可能把视觉目标变成可执行动作。

The report trains its IDM on roughly 400 hours of robot data including task-agnostic play, with human egocentric and NEO data aligning the video model [53]. No robot demonstration of a task differs from no robot data anywhere in the system. The IDM must ground the body’s motion.

视觉未来也未必能唯一决定动作：相似的画面可以由不同受力、抓取或碰撞过程产生。单目视频尤其难确定深度和接触状态。选择视频作为接口，扩大了可利用的预训练数据，同时把部分可执行性判断交给了 IDM 和闭环反馈。

Visual futures do not uniquely identify action: similar images can arise from different forces, grasps or contacts. Monocular depth adds ambiguity. Video interfaces expand usable pretraining data while assigning feasibility to inverse dynamics and feedback.

4速度与生成质量怎样影响闭环Latency and generative quality in control

2026 年一月报告给出的系统约需十一秒生成五秒视频，另需约一秒 IDM 推理，并讨论未来的闭环重规划 [53]。这组数字对应当时的系统，说明视频生成能力与快速接触反应之间仍有距离。它不应被描述成已经具备低时延的连续反应控制。

The January 2026 report describes about eleven seconds to generate five seconds of video and another second for IDM inference, discussing closed-loop replanning as future work [53]. These are release-specific figures, illustrating the gap between video generation and fast contact reactions.

更好的生成质量可以减少明显错误的动作意图，但多次采样选最好结果也会增加等待和选择成本。评测必须说明是否使用人工筛选、多少次候选生成，以及时间从哪一步开始计算；只统计最终执行片段会隐藏这些成本。

Better generations can reduce bad action intent, while selecting among candidates adds latency and selection cost. Report manual filtering, candidate counts and when the timer starts; measuring only the final execution hides preparation.

5与 Dyna、GR00T 2 的不同Contrast Dyna and GR00T 2

1XWM 的视频 → IDM、Dyna-2 和 GR00T 2 的联合视频动作建模，争论的是动作与世界预测如何耦合。分开学习便于复用视频生成器，却引入转换误差；联合学习可以对齐两种输出，但需要更复杂的混合训练。最终应比较闭环任务、计算预算与迁移，而不是只比较生成视频质量。

1XWM’s video-to-IDM pipeline and joint modeling in Dyna-2 and GR00T 2 differ in coupling. Separate stages reuse video generators but add conversion error; joint learning aligns outputs with a more complex recipe. Compare physical tasks, compute and transfer, beyond visual quality.

推荐先读 2025 年评测报告，再读 2026 年策略报告。把“世界模型是评测器”与“世界模型参与策略”分清，可以避免将预测准确率写成机器人的自主能力。

Read the 2025 evaluator report before the 2026 policy report. Keeping these roles separate prevents prediction quality from being mistaken for robot autonomy.
