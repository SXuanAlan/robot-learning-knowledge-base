Generalist

GEN 系列围绕一个问题展开：直接扩大物理交互经验，能否同时获得可靠技能和快速适应新任务的能力？GEN-0、GEN-1 与 GEN-1.5 分别提供了不同层次的证据。

The GEN series asks whether scaling physical interaction can produce both reliable skills and fast task adaptation. GEN-0, GEN-1 and GEN-1.5 offer evidence at different levels.

技术路线的连续性在于先建立可复用的物理经验，而不是每次从零训练专用策略。真正的考验是：增加预训练以后，下游需要多少数据、达到什么可靠性、在什么分布上成立？这比单独报告模型大小更接近机器人基础模型的价值。

The continuity is reusable physical experience instead of a new specialist for every task. The test is whether pretraining reduces downstream data requirements at a useful reliability level across a specified distribution. That is more meaningful than parameter count alone.

2采集系统也是模型的一部分The collection system shapes the model

GEN-1 报告强调从自采物理交互数据出发训练，而不是直接把一个预训练 VLM 接上动作头；其报告规模达到约五十万小时 [15]。但人的交互数据与目标机器人的动作仍有对应关系要解决，后训练数据就是这部分成本的重要入口。

GEN-1 emphasizes training from its own physical-interaction corpus rather than attaching actions to a pretrained VLM; the release reports roughly half a million hours [15]. Human experience still needs grounding in the target robot, making adaptation data an important part of the cost.

这与 PI 的主要区别在学习起点，而非“一个有语义、一个没有语义”。物理交互同样可以承载任务意图。应比较在匹配数据与计算预算下，两种初始化对新物体、精细接触和语言指令的收益；单看各自最擅长的 Demo 无法隔离架构作用。

The distinction from PI concerns the learning foundation, not whether one system has semantics. Physical interactions encode intent too. Matched data and compute are needed to isolate differences in object transfer, precise contact and language following.

3GEN-1：把成功率和速度一起看GEN-1: reliability alongside speed

GEN-1 把可靠性、速度和意外情况下的恢复并列为 mastery，并报告在若干简单任务上经过少量机器人数据适配后的高成功率 [15]。这种评价比“做成一次”更接近应用，但仍要知道任务分布：固定工位反复操作，与在新家庭识别杂物并完成整套流程，是不同的负担。

GEN-1 frames mastery as reliability, speed and improvisation, reporting strong performance on several simple tasks after modest robot adaptation [15]. That improves on a single success, but repeated work at one station differs from interpreting clutter and completing a workflow in a new home.

连续视频最值得读的是失败如何恢复、动作是否变慢、是否有人补料和清场。即使单次操作达到很高成功率，也要看错误是否独立、是否会累积，以及一个错误能否让后续任务无法继续。吞吐量应包含重置和人工服务时间。

In continuous footage, inspect recovery, speed drift, replenishment and clearing. High single-operation reliability leaves questions about correlated errors and failures that block later work. Throughput should account for reset and human service time.

4GEN-1.5：一次示范改变了什么GEN-1.5: what one demonstration changes

GEN-1.5 的报告明确区分两种设置：十个短任务的一次上下文示范，平均成功率 59%；每任务五分钟数据、十次梯度更新的 few-shot 设置，平均 83% [20]。它还描述约三十秒观测记忆与 100 Hz 动作轨迹输出。轨迹采样频率不等于整个大模型每秒前向推理一百次。

GEN-1.5 distinguishes one-shot prompting on ten short tasks, averaging 59%, from five minutes of task data and ten gradient steps, averaging 83% [20]. It describes about 30 seconds of observation memory and 100 Hz action trajectories. Trajectory rate is not the model’s forward-pass frequency.

与 Skild S1 比较时，真正有区分力的是：测试任务是否在预训练中出现，是否只是新物体或顺序，任务有多长，以及一次示范能否改变已经熟悉的行为。One-shot 是输入数量，泛化强度还取决于测试与训练相距多远。

Against Skild S1, distinguish unseen tasks from new objects or ordering, inspect horizons, and ask whether demonstrations can change a familiar behavior. One-shot counts the examples; generalization also depends on the distance from training.

5技术主张与验证条件Technical claims and validation conditions

Generalist 的路线押注是：足够丰富的交互预训练能降低下游适配成本，并逐步形成上下文学习能力。需要继续看的不是更多独立短片，而是按任务新颖度分组的统一评测、反复任务中的失败记录，以及采集设备变化之后能力是否仍然保持。公开报告尚未完整给出可复现架构和训练配方，不能据此反推所有内部模块。

The bet is that broad physical pretraining lowers adaptation cost and supports context learning. Stronger evidence would group tests by novelty, expose failures across repetitions, and test changes to capture hardware. Public reports do not disclose a fully reproducible architecture and training recipe.
