1共同的研究起点，不同的公司答案Shared research roots, different company answers

这些公司并不是互不相干的技术岛屿。Tony Zhao 是 Chelsea Finn 的学生，如今与 Cheng Chi 创办 Sunday；Finn 是 Physical Intelligence 的联合创始人；Zipeng Fu 同样师从 Finn，如今是 Reward AI 的联合创始人兼 CEO [31, 32, 33, 34]。Cheng Chi 则师从 Shuran Song [32]。师承解释了问题意识与共同积累，但不能直接推出公司间的合作关系或技术完全一致。

These companies share research histories. Tony Zhao studied with Chelsea Finn and co-founded Sunday with Cheng Chi; Finn co-founded Physical Intelligence. Zipeng Fu, another Finn advisee, is Reward AI’s co-founder and CEO [31, 33, 34]. Cheng Chi was advised by Shuran Song [32]. These connections explain shared research experience, not corporate partnerships or identical technology.

Mobile ALOHA 是这个交叉网络里很好的起点：Fu、Zhao、Finn 把低成本双臂示范扩展到移动操作，并研究与静态 ALOHA 数据联合训练 [30]。把它与 Cheng Chi 的 Diffusion Policy 和 UMI 放在一起读，会看到两条问题线索汇合：一条是怎样学到复杂的动作序列，另一条是怎样让示范走出固定实验台 [1, 3]。

Mobile ALOHA is a useful starting point: Fu, Zhao and Finn extend low-cost bimanual demonstrations to mobile manipulation and co-train with static ALOHA data [30]. Read alongside Chi’s Diffusion Policy and UMI, it connects learning complex action sequences with collecting demonstrations beyond a fixed lab bench [1, 3].

由此可以理解一个有趣的变化：做过低成本 Teleop 的研究者，后来也会尝试减少对 Teleop 的依赖。这不是简单地否定早期工作，而是在更大采集规模下重新分配硬件、人的时间和控制工程成本。下面的技术主旨是本文对公开工作的归纳，不是仅凭师承推测创业动机。

Researchers who built low-cost teleoperation can later seek to reduce its use. Scaling changes the trade-off between hardware, human time and control engineering. The interpretations below are drawn from published work, rather than inferred founder motives.

2人的数据：成本移到了哪里Human data: where the cost moves

Sunday、Generalist 与 Reward 都把人的物理交互放到数据叙事中心，但对“转移给机器人什么”有不同选择。Sunday 的 ACT-1 把 Skill Capture Glove 与 Memo 的手协同设计 [12]；Generalist 的 GEN 系列强调扩大自采交互数据与模型规模 [9, 15]；Reward 的 OM-1 则突出带触觉的 Omnibody Hand 及跨本体执行 [22]。

Sunday, Generalist and Reward all center human physical interaction, but transfer different interfaces to the robot. Sunday co-designs its capture glove with Memo’s hand [12]; Generalist emphasizes interaction-data and model scale [9, 15]; Reward emphasizes tactile Omnibody Hands and execution across bodies [22].

这三种叙事共享一个经济问题：怎样让人高效采集，同时不丢掉机器人执行需要的信息？更一致的相机与手部几何减少视觉和动作映射的负担；触觉补充接触信息；跨本体控制则要承担不同机器人间的执行差异。所谓“Zero Robot Data”应回到具体模型、阶段和控制层来读，不能自动推广成整个机器人系统没有用过机器人知识或仿真。

The shared economic question is how people can collect efficiently without losing information needed for execution. Matched cameras and hands ease mapping; touch adds contact information; cross-body control handles execution differences. “Zero robot data” must be interpreted for the specified model and training stage, not extended automatically to every controller or component.

从 Mobile ALOHA 到 Reward，可以看到“怎样把人的技能交给机器人”这个问题的延续；从 UMI 到 Sunday，可以看到采集接口与执行接口协同设计的延续。但 OM-1 并不是 Mobile ALOHA 的直接产品化：Reward 自己将 Omnibody Hand 的技术脉络连接到 DexCap [4, 22]。共同作者与系统来源是两种不同的关系。

Mobile ALOHA and Reward share a concern with transferring human skill; UMI and Sunday share an emphasis on capture–execution interfaces. Yet OM-1 is not simply a productized Mobile ALOHA: Reward identifies DexCap in the Omnibody Hand’s technical lineage [4, 22]. Co-authorship and system provenance are different relationships.

3模型之争：先学语言、交互还是动态Model priors: language, interaction or dynamics

PI 的 π 系列从视觉语言模型出发学习动作 [5]，Generalist 的 GEN-1 强调从物理交互数据建立模型 [15]，Dyna-2 则用视频预测与动作相结合的 WAM 路线 [18]。三者争论的是预训练归纳偏置：语言语义、物理交互序列和视觉动态，哪一种更值得成为学习的出发点？

PI’s π series learns actions from a vision-language foundation [5]; Generalist’s GEN-1 emphasizes a foundation built on physical interaction [15]; Dyna-2 uses a world-action model combining video prediction and action [18]. The debate concerns the starting inductive bias: language semantics, physical sequences or visual dynamics.

但“VLA = Teleop，WAM = 人类视频”并不成立。PI 已公开用 Egocentric 人类视频提升 VLA 的研究，且迁移能力依赖机器人数据的规模 [36]。因此，架构与数据来源是两个轴；混合数据也不是折中方案的同义词，而可能是用一种数据补另一种数据缺失的监督。

The equation “VLA means teleop, WAM means human video” is misleading. PI has published human-video transfer in VLAs, with transfer depending on robot-data scale [36]. Architecture and data source are separate axes; mixtures can supply supervision missing from either source alone.

Google DeepMind 的 Gemini Robotics 与 NVIDIA 的 GR00T 也属于这张图谱；NVIDIA 同时参与 DreamZero 和 SONIC 这样的不同层次工作 [7, 14, 17, 10]。给公司贴单一架构标签会漏掉内部并行路线。更可比较的单位是某次发布的模型、动作接口和评测设置。

Gemini Robotics and GR00T belong in this comparison; NVIDIA also contributes work at different levels, including DreamZero and SONIC [7, 14, 17, 10]. A single architecture label can miss parallel efforts inside a company. The useful unit is a particular release, action interface and evaluation.

Figure 自身也在改变模型起点：Helix 02 从预训练 VLM 出发，Helix 2.5 则从随机初始化开始，在 Index 上预训练 [40]。同一家公司的不同发布，可能已经跨过了这里的路线边界。

Figure itself changes its starting point: Helix 02 uses a pretrained VLM, whereas Helix 2.5 is initialized randomly and pretrained on Index [40]. A company can cross these architectural boundaries between releases.

NVIDIA 最新的 GR00T 2 已进一步采用联合视频与动作预测，并连接 SONIC 全身控制 [50]。这也是按具体发布而不是给公司贴固定标签的原因。

4本体与控制：谁承担迁移差异Bodies and control: absorbing mismatch

Figure 的 Helix 02、Dyna-2.1 和 Reward OM-1 都说明，数据路线最终必须落到控制接口上 [13, 23, 22]。高层输出的动作目标，只有被低层控制器稳定执行后才有意义。人的示范可以训练上层，仿真 RL 可以训练下层，两件事并不矛盾。

Helix 02, Dyna-2.1 and OM-1 all show why a data strategy must reach a control interface [13, 23, 22]. High-level action targets matter only if lower-level control executes them reliably. Human demonstrations at the upper layer and simulated RL below can coexist.

本文的比较是：把差异吸收到硬件、动作表示还是控制层，会改变数据的复用方式。更相似的采集与执行硬件可能让迁移更容易；更通用的本体范围则要求控制接口承受更多差异。真正的取舍是覆盖、控制难度与部署成本，而不是有腿或有轮子本身能证明模型更通用。

The analytical trade-off is where mismatch is absorbed: hardware, action representation or control. Matched capture and execution can ease transfer; broader embodiments demand more from the interface. Coverage, control difficulty and deployment cost matter more than treating legs or wheels as proof of model generality.

5Sunday × Figure：从材质到可靠性Sunday × Figure: from materials to reliability

5.1 一月：塑料与玻璃，接触是否足够稳5.1 January: plastic, glass and contact tolerance

2026 年 1 月 27 日，Tony Zhao 评论 Figure 的 Helix 02 洗碗机演示：先肯定 Whole-Body Control，再问这次物体看起来是否都是塑料，部分操作是否依赖这种材质 [37]。1 月 28 日，Brett Adcock 发布操作玻璃器皿的视频回应，并称片段由 Helix 02 自主执行 [38]。两条原帖及其引用关系可在当时报道的嵌入记录中交叉查看 [39]。

On January 27, 2026, Tony Zhao praised the whole-body control in Figure’s dishwasher demo, then questioned whether the objects were plastic and whether some manipulations relied on that material [37]. Brett Adcock responded the next day with glassware footage described as autonomous Helix 02 execution [38]. The contemporary report embeds the exchange [39].

争论的技术内核是接触容错。从机械操作角度看，塑料与玻璃在重量、摩擦、刚度及容许碰撞程度上可能不同；一条在宽容物体上成功的轨迹，未必能原样迁移。Tony 提出的是演示条件的问题，不能把它改写成对造假的指控。

The technical issue is contact tolerance. Weight, friction, stiffness and tolerance for collision can differ across objects; a trajectory that works on a forgiving object need not transfer unchanged. Zhao questioned demonstration conditions, which is not an allegation of fabrication.

玻璃视频回应了“能否处理这类物体”的一部分疑问，但一个精选片段不能直接给出跨材质成功率。要判断两条路线的可靠性，需要重复次数、物体与摆放分布、人工干预以及失败恢复。同样，这场交锋也没有证明 Sunday 或 Figure 的整体方案胜出；它揭示的是双方都必须面对的评测问题。

The response addresses whether glassware can be handled in the shown situation, but a selected clip does not establish reliability across materials. Repetitions, object and placement distributions, interventions and recovery remain relevant. The exchange exposes an evaluation question rather than establishing an overall winner.

5.2 九月：237/420，怎样才算有用5.2 September: 237/420 — what counts as useful?

第二次交锋发生在 2026 年 9 月 17 日。Figure 发布 Helix 2.5，Brett Adcock 强调机器人能够在未见过的住宅工作 [40, 42]。Tony 随即引用这条发布帖，抓住报告中的 237/420 次成功批评其距离“真正有用”仍有差距，并写下：“Doing useful work = generalization + reliability.” [41]。这次争论的中心从物体材质转到了部署可靠性。

The second exchange occurred on September 17, 2026. Figure released Helix 2.5, and Brett Adcock emphasized work in unfamiliar homes [40, 42]. Zhao quoted the announcement, pointing to 237 successes in 420 attempts and arguing that useful work requires both generalization and reliability [41]. The focus had shifted from object material to deployment reliability.

Figure 的 Zero-Shot 指向未见住宅与物体：三种行为先用其他地点的数据训练，再在 30 个新住宅评测；每种任务使用固定 Checkpoint。一次叠毛巾成功要求四条都完成，整理玩具要求全部收好，铺床要求整个任务完成 [40]。因此，Tony 所说的“差不多一半失败”是在读完整任务统计，不是从剪辑视频猜测失败率。

Figure’s zero-shot claim concerns unseen homes and objects: the three behaviors were trained elsewhere, then evaluated in 30 homes with a fixed checkpoint per task. A successful towel trial required all four towels; toy tidying and bed making likewise required the complete job [40]. Zhao’s criticism therefore concerned full-task statistics, rather than a failure rate inferred from edited footage.

Tony 的立场也与 Sunday 更早发布的 ACT-2 叙事相连。7 月 17 日的报告把可靠性放在核心，提出在明确任务范围与适配成本下衡量能力的 Solve 标准，报告单件衣物折叠并堆放成功 778/785 次，即 99.1% [43]。但这个数字不能直接与 Figure 的 56.4% 排名：两边的一次试验包含的工作量、物体与成功标准不同。

Zhao’s position connects to Sunday’s earlier ACT-2 report, published July 17. Its Solve framework evaluates reliability within a declared scope and adaptation cost; it reports 778/785 successful individual-garment folds and stacks, or 99.1% [43]. That number cannot directly rank against Figure’s 56.4%: trial units, objects and success criteria differ.

报告Report一次成功的单位Unit of success公开结果Reported result
Figure · Helix 2.5 [40]完整家务任务；毛巾任务要求四条全部完成A complete chore; towel trials require all four towels237/420 · 56.4%
Sunday · ACT-2 [43]单件衣物折叠并堆放；评测覆盖九类衣物One garment folded and stacked; evaluation spans nine garment types778/785 · 99.1%

这次交锋揭示了两个不同问题：训练得到的能力有没有迁移到新环境，以及迁移后的绝对成功率是否足以让用户交付任务。前者可以有实质进步，后者仍可能不足。本文因此把九月争论读作对“泛化进步”与“可交付能力”之间距离的争论，而不是据此断言模仿学习无法泛化，或哪家公司已经解决家务机器人。

The exchange exposes two questions: does a learned capability transfer to new settings, and is its absolute reliability high enough for a user to entrust the task? Transfer can improve while deployment remains inadequate. This is a debate about the distance between generalization progress and dependable utility, rather than proof that imitation learning cannot generalize or that either company has solved home robotics.

6Generalist × Skild：One-Shot 的边界Generalist × Skild: what counts as one-shot

另一场更直接写进技术报告的分歧，发生在 Generalist GEN-1.5 与 Skild S1 的 One-Shot / In-Context Learning 叙事中。Generalist 强调预训练后涌现的一次示范适应，也明确说明展示的任务简单且短时程 [20]；Skild 则点名相关工作偏向短任务或预训练分布内任务，并强调自己的未见任务与长时程设置 [19]。

A second disagreement appears in technical reports on GEN-1.5 and Skild S1. Generalist emphasizes one-example adaptation emerging from pretraining and explicitly describes its shown tasks as simple and short-horizon [20]. Skild contrasts related work with its unseen-task and long-horizon setting [19].

这不是同一张榜单上的两个分数。One-Shot 可能指不给梯度更新的 Context Prompt，也可能指极少示范后的 Fine-tuning；“新任务”也可能是新物体、新组合，或训练中未有的技能。Generalist 同时报告了不更新权重的适应和少量梯度步的适应 [20]，比较时必须把两者分开。

These are not two scores on one leaderboard. One-shot can refer to a context prompt without weight updates or to fine-tuning with very few examples. Novelty can mean objects, compositions or skills. Generalist reports both no-update adaptation and adaptation with a few gradient steps [20]; those results must remain separate.

本文据此把争论转成一个可检验的问题：在相同预训练边界、相同任务时长与相同成功定义下，一段新示范到底增加了多少能力？短任务的适应性和长任务的稳定性都重要，但回答的是不同的问题。

A testable comparison would hold pretraining boundaries, task horizon and success definitions fixed, then measure the capability added by one new demonstration. Short-task adaptation and long-task reliability both matter, but answer different questions.
