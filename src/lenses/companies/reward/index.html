Reward AI

OM-1 不只是“用人的示范来训练”。它把采集工具、感知、动作接口和不同机器人的控制层当作一整个系统来设计，看它的 Demo 也要结合这套接口来看。

OM-1 is more than a claim about human demonstrations. It co-designs capture tools, sensing, action interfaces and controllers across robot bodies. Its launch demos make sense in that system context.

1OM-1 原始演示与技术目标Original OM-1 demonstrations and technical objectives

OM-1 的发布材料展示了打包、操作连接件、搬运和在不同机器人上执行 [22]。看这些片段时，与其问“像不像人”，不如问：采集时记录下了哪些接触信息？换一台机器人以后，哪些动作意图的含义没有变？跨本体的剪辑说明系统能支持不同的身体，但光看剪辑，判断不了每种机器人是不是完全没做适配。

The OM-1 release shows packaging, connector manipulation, handling and cross-body execution [22]. Ask what contact information is captured, and what action meaning survives a body change. Cross-body footage demonstrates supported embodiments, but a montage alone cannot establish the absence of adaptation.

Zipeng Fu 和 Tony Zhao、Chelsea Finn 合作的 Mobile ALOHA，研究的是移动双臂的示范 [30, 33]；而 Reward 的手部系统明确说是从 DexCap 发展来的 [4, 22]。要分清研究者长期关心的问题和产品具体的技术来源：人的技能怎么表示，和怎么低成本地拿到这种表示，是相关但不同的两个问题。

Zipeng Fu’s Mobile ALOHA work with Tony Zhao and Chelsea Finn studies mobile bimanual demonstrations [30, 33]. Reward connects its hand system specifically to DexCap [4, 22]. A researcher’s continuing interests and a product’s technical provenance are related, distinct connections.

2Omnibody Hand：对齐的是接口Omnibody Hand: aligning an interface

Omnibody Hand 把功能性的手部自由度、触觉、接近感知和手内相机都集成在同一个接口里，再结合视觉惯性和电磁位姿追踪 [22]。这些设计分别补充了手的形状、接触前的距离、接触后的反馈和运动轨迹，这些信息光靠 Egocentric RGB 是没法稳定恢复出来的。

Omnibody Hand combines functional hand motion, touch, proximity and in-hand cameras with visual-inertial and electromagnetic tracking [22]. These signals capture geometry, approach, contact and trajectories. They are not interchangeable with what egocentric RGB alone can reliably reveal.

这条路线把成本从遥控机器人，转到了可穿戴设备的标定、维护和数据质量控制上。好处是动作和感知对得更齐；代价是数据离不开这套特定的工具，人戴上它以后，动作也不完全是自然的了。所以说“人的数据更便宜”时，还要一起算上有效样本的比例、佩戴的负担，以及接触信息记录得准不准。

The cost moves from operating robots remotely to calibration, maintenance and quality control of wearable devices. Alignment improves, but data depends on the tool and demonstrations may change when people wear it. The relevant economics include usable-data yield, operator burden and fidelity of contact signals.

3OM-1 和底层控制怎样分工What OM-1 and the controller each do

官方说 OM-1 的策略只从人的数据中学习，不同模态的数据各自保留原来的采样频率；机器人的底层控制则另外用仿真 RL 训练，并且独立于上层推理运行 [22]。所以“没有用机器人训练数据”说的是策略学习这一个阶段，不能理解成整套系统里没有机器人模型、没训练过控制器、也没做系统辨识。

The release describes OM-1 policy learning from human data with modality histories at their native rates, while a separate low-level controller uses simulated RL and runs independently [22]. The data claim needs a stage-specific interpretation; it does not remove robot models or control engineering.

多速率设计是为了不把快速的触觉反馈降到慢速的视觉帧率。但真正的反应速度取决于整条链路：传感器到模型的延迟、策略输出的是什么、控制器多久更新一次、轨迹怎么切换。传感器快，闭环不一定快；所以报告里讲的控制分工，比一个“端到端”的标签更能说明问题。

A multi-rate design can preserve fast contact signals that would be lost at camera frame rate. Actual reaction speed still depends on sensing latency, the action interface, controller updates and trajectory transitions. A fast sensor alone does not make a fast loop.

4接触任务：看策略怎样衔接各个阶段Contact tasks: inspect transitions

拧开连接件和拔网线是两种不同的接触：前者要在约束下持续旋转，后者要处理卡扣和拔出那一瞬间。示范有没有覆盖这些状态，比任务的名字更能说明模型学到了什么。想验证触觉到底有多大用，就要比较：去掉触觉以后表现如何，接触变化时能不能恢复，同样训练预算下换材质的结果怎样。

Unscrewing and unplugging expose different contact structures: constrained rotation versus latching and sudden release. Coverage of those states is more informative than a task label. To isolate touch, examine ablations, recovery under changed contacts and material transfer at matched training budgets.

官方还给出了高速追踪时的超调对比，以及只采少量数据就学会新任务的展示 [22]。追踪误差是传感和估计的指标，替代不了闭环成功率；采集新任务数据的用时，也不包括硬件研发和模型训练的成本。这条路线最终要看的，是这些部件加在一起，有没有降低完整任务的失败率。

The report also includes tracking-overshoot measurements and short task-data collection examples [22]. Estimation error is not task success, and collection time is not total development cost. The system-level question is whether the components jointly reduce complete-task failures.

这张表是技术上的对照，不是性能排名。接下来最有价值的证据会是：同一个任务在不同机器人上的适配成本、传感器的消融实验，以及包含失败和复位的连续运行记录。现有的 OM-1 材料还不足以复现整个训练系统，没公开的网络细节就留白，不必用熟悉的 VLA 或 diffusion 标签去补。

This is an interpretation, not a ranking. Useful next evidence includes adaptation budgets across bodies, sensor ablations, and continuous logs including failures and resets. The public OM-1 material is insufficient to reproduce the full training system; undisclosed details should not be filled in with familiar architecture labels.
