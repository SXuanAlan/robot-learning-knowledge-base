1任务命令、控制接口与接触动力学Task commands, control interfaces and contact dynamics

四足、双足和 humanoid 面对的是同一个控制难题：身体有惯性，脚和地面的接触不断切换，浮动基座没法直接驱动，手臂的动作和负载还会反过来影响平衡。策略到底输出关节力矩、PD target、末端目标，还是可复用的技能指令，决定了学习要扛下哪一层的复杂度。

Quadrupeds, bipeds and humanoids share inertial bodies, changing support contacts and an underactuated floating base. Arm motion and payload also affect balance. Choosing torques, PD targets, end-effector targets or skill commands determines how much complexity the learned policy must handle.

M(q)q¨+C(q,q˙)+g(q)=Bτ+Jc(q)⊤λτPD=Kp(qdes−q)−Kdq˙\begin{aligned} M(q)\ddot q+C(q,\dot q)+g(q)&=B\tau+J_c(q)^\top\lambda\\ \tau_{\mathrm{PD}}&=K_p(q_{\mathrm{des}}-q)-K_d\dot q \end{aligned}

第一行说的是，动作必须满足动力学和接触约束；第二行说的是，常见的 policy 输出的是位置目标，真正的力矩由更快的局部反馈算出来。PD target 不等于关节实际到达的位置，接触时的 tracking error 还会影响交互力。模型推理的频率和电机控制的频率要分开看。

The first equation expresses dynamics and contact coupling. The second shows why a policy can output position targets while a faster local loop produces torques. Targets differ from achieved positions, and tracking errors influence contact forces. Policy inference rate and motor-loop rate are distinct.

2四足与双足运动：鲁棒性与自适应Quadruped and biped locomotion: robustness and adaptation

最基本的速度跟踪任务，输入是机身速度和转向指令，策略根据姿态、关节状态、历史动作和重力方向来控制步态。最后学出什么样的步态，取决于训练时的 terrain curriculum、对摩擦、质量和时延的随机化，以及奖励里的能耗、打滑和姿态项。仿真并行让 PPO 每次都要重新采样的成本变得可以接受，但 reward 设计不好，照样会学出钻空子的怪步态。

Basic locomotion follows body-velocity and yaw commands from proprioception and action history. Terrain curricula, randomization of friction, mass and delay, and energy/slip/posture rewards shape the gait. Parallel simulation makes PPO’s fresh-rollout cost practical, but misspecified rewards can still produce undesirable behavior.

RMA：根据历史观测在线适应RMA: online adaptation from observation history

RMA 把 base policy 和 adaptation module 拆开：训练时，用仿真里已知的环境参数学一个 latent 条件；部署时，根据最近的 Proprioception 和动作历史去估计这个条件，从而适应不同的地面和负载 [63]。这里的 online adaptation 指的是推断隐藏条件，并不是边走边对 policy 做梯度更新。

RMA separates a base policy from an adaptation module. Training uses simulated environment parameters to learn a latent condition; deployment estimates that condition from recent proprioception and action history [63]. Online adaptation here primarily means inference of hidden conditions, not gradient updates to the walking policy.

这也说明了视觉和触地反馈为什么互补：相机能提前看到下一步踩在哪，Proprioception 则在脚落地之后告诉你真实的动力学。双足的支撑面更窄，单脚支撑的时间更多；humanoid 的腰、手和负载还会改变质心和角动量，所以四足的 reward 和 action space 不能原样搬过来。

Vision anticipates footholds while proprioception reveals dynamics after contact. Bipeds have narrower support and frequent single-foot stance; a humanoid’s torso, arms and payload also affect center of mass and momentum. Quadruped rewards and action spaces cannot simply be copied unchanged.

3人体动作估计、Retargeting 与机器人跟踪Human-motion estimation, retargeting and robot tracking

GVHMR 负责把相机运动和人体运动分开、保证重力方向一致，并恢复全局轨迹；但视频里的人体关节还不是机器人的关节。GMR 再处理身体比例、关节拓扑、姿态限制和位置关系，把动作转成机器人能用的 reference。最后由 tracking RL 学习如何在重力、地面接触和外界扰动下真正把动作做出来。

GVHMR recovers world-grounded human motion despite camera motion. Human joints are not robot joints. GMR addresses morphology, topology, limits and spatial relationships to build a robot reference. Tracking RL then learns execution under gravity, contact and disturbances.

参考动作看起来像人，并不是唯一的标准。坐椅子、开门、攀爬这类动作还依赖场景几何和接触时序，只恢复人体骨架，就把椅子、门把手这些支撑物丢掉了。清洗数据时要检查接触、速度、关节限位和与环境的关系，而不是只看渲染出来顺不顺。

Human-like appearance is insufficient. Sitting, opening doors and climbing depend on scene geometry and contact timing that a body skeleton alone omits. Inspect contacts, velocities, joint limits and environment relationships rather than only smooth rendering.

4OmniRetarget：保留交互关系的 RetargetingOmniRetarget: interaction-preserving motion retargeting

人体姿态能映射到机器人上，不代表人和环境的交互关系也保住了。搬箱子时手要贴着箱面，上台阶时脚要踩在台沿上；如果只是把人体关节位置按比例缩放过去，很容易出现穿模、滑脚或者接触丢失。OmniRetarget [109] 把机器人、物体和地形之间的相对关系一起放进了优化问题。

Mapping body poses does not automatically preserve interactions. Hands must remain on a box and feet on a platform; matching scaled human keypoints can produce penetration, skating or lost contact. OmniRetarget [109] optimizes relationships among the robot, objects and terrain together.

Interaction mesh：保持相对几何与接触关系Interaction mesh: preserve relative geometry and contact

具体做法是把身体关键点、物体表面点和地形点连成一张 interaction mesh，用 Laplacian 坐标描述每个点相对邻居的偏移。优化时让机器人这一侧的相对结构尽量接近人这一侧，同时加上不穿透、关节和速度限制以及接触约束。这样目标就从“复制绝对位置”变成了“在机器人身上保留和任务有关的相对关系”。

An interaction mesh connects body landmarks, object surfaces and terrain samples. Laplacian coordinates encode each point relative to its neighbors. Optimization preserves this structure while enforcing kinematic and contact constraints. The objective is task-relevant relational geometry on the target body, rather than copying absolute positions.

L(pi)=pi−∑j∈𝒩(i)wijpjEinteraction=∑i∥L(pihuman)−L(pirobot)∥22\begin{aligned}L(p_i)&=p_i-\sum_{j\in\mathcal N(i)}w_{ij}p_j\\E_{\mathrm{interaction}}&=\sum_i\left\|L(p_i^{\mathrm{human}})-L(p_i^{\mathrm{robot}})\right\|_2^2\end{aligned}

这套方法顺便还能做数据增强：改一下物体的位姿、大小或者地形高度，再重新求解一条满足这些关系和约束的机器人轨迹。增强出来的是可行的参考动作，而不是把画面平移一下、给关节角加点噪声。作者用这些参考训练了只用 Proprioception 的 RL 策略，展示了与场景的交互和搬运。它并不是一个拿任意 RGB 视频就能直接上机的端到端策略。

Changing object poses, sizes or terrain heights and solving again produces augmented feasible references. This differs from image transformations or arbitrary joint noise. The authors train proprioceptive RL trackers from these references for scene interaction and carrying; the method is not a direct arbitrary-RGB-to-hardware policy.

它和 GMR 的分工大致是：GMR 把人体动作在运动学上映射到机器人；OmniRetarget 在生成参考时进一步考虑了与场景的交互；BeyondMimic、SuperMimic 则学习让身体去跟踪这些参考。三者可以是同一个系统里的不同环节，没法用一张 tracking 分数表来比较。

GMR maps human motion into robot kinematics; OmniRetarget additionally models scene interactions during reference generation; BeyondMimic / SuperMimic learn reference tracking. They can occupy different stages in one system and should not be collapsed into a single tracking leaderboard.

5DeepMimic、AMP 与 BeyondMimicDeepMimic, AMP and BeyondMimic

AMP [62]motion transition discriminator + task rewardMotion-transition discriminator + task reward保留动作分布的风格，不逐帧绑定一个片段Preserve motion style without framewise binding to a clip

DeepMimic 把 reference 的 phase 告诉策略，用姿态、速度、末端和质心的误差构造 imitation reward，再叠加任务目标。AMP 则用一个 discriminator 判断状态转移像不像 motion dataset 里的动作，不要求和某个参考时刻一一对应。前者追求忠实地跟踪，后者追求风格上像；AMP 动作自然，但并不能精确复现任意指定的姿势。

DeepMimic conditions on reference phase and rewards matching pose, velocity and related motion features alongside task objectives. AMP evaluates motion-transition style with a discriminator instead of aligning every frame to a fixed clip. Distributional naturalness does not guarantee exact tracking of an arbitrary requested pose.

BeyondMimic 先用高质量的 tracking 得到状态和动作序列，再把它们蒸馏成一个生成模型，测试时用 cost 去引导它导航或避障。这说明 motion tracker 是把参考动作变成可执行技能的基础设施；要泛化到任务控制，还得决定什么时候用哪个动作、怎么衔接、什么时候偏离参考。能做出一段高难度动作，不代表模型理解了新任务。

BeyondMimic distills tracking rollouts into a generative state-action model and uses test-time costs for task guidance. A tracker is infrastructure for executable skills; task control additionally needs selection, composition and useful deviations from reference. A difficult motion alone does not establish new-task understanding.

HOVER、SONIC 这类做通用接口的工作，要解决的都是同一个问题：来自不同来源的指令，能不能落到一个统一、稳定的底层控制上。比较它们时，要看 command space、用了多少历史观测、teacher 和 student 各能看到什么，以及新 motion 和新任务是怎么划分的 [10]。

General interfaces such as HOVER and SONIC ask how diverse commands map into stable low-level behavior. Compare command spaces, observation histories, teacher/student information and the distinction between unseen motions and unseen tasks [10].

6SuperMimic：借助可微 World Model 学全身控制SuperMimic: learning whole-body control through a differentiable world model

从 DeepMimic 一次学一个动作，到 BeyondMimic 的专家加通用跟踪器，再到 SONIC [10] 的大规模通用控制，背后一直有个问题：学很多动作的训练成本怎么分摊。SuperMimic [110] 的思路是，不同的 motion 共用同一台机器人的动力学，所以先从仿真交互里学一个可微的动力学代理模型，再让策略通过这个模型来学。

From individual skills in DeepMimic to BeyondMimic’s experts/generalist tracker and SONIC’s scaled general control [10], a central question is how to amortize training across motions. SuperMimic [110] exploits shared robot dynamics by fitting a differentiable surrogate from simulation and improving the policy through it.

训练循环：模型拟合与策略改进交替进行Training loop: alternate model fitting and policy improvement

当前策略在 MuJoCo Warp 里采集 transition，存进 replay buffer；模型用短时间窗口去拟合状态、观测、奖励和终止信号。更新策略时，从 replay 里取一个状态出发，在模型里展开一段短的 imagined rollout，用 BPTT 把回报的梯度传回动作和策略参数，窗口之外的价值交给 critic 估计。模型只在这一步冻结参数，之后仍然会用新的交互数据继续更新。

The policy collects transitions in MuJoCo Warp into replay. Short windows fit state, observation, reward and termination quantities. Policy improvement starts from replay states, unrolls imagined trajectories, backpropagates through actions and uses a critic beyond the finite horizon. Model parameters are frozen during this update, then fitted again on new experience.

s^k+1=fω(s^k,ak,ξ),ak=πθ(o^k,gk)J^(θ)=𝔼 ⁣[∑k=0H−1γkr^k+γHVψ(s^H)]\begin{aligned}\widehat s_{k+1}&=f_\omega(\widehat s_k,a_k,\xi),\quad a_k=\pi_\theta(\widehat o_k,g_k)\\\widehat J(\theta)&=\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}\gamma^k\widehat r_k+\gamma^H V_\psi(\widehat s_H)\right]\end{aligned}

上式省略了终止和生存权重，只为突出梯度的路径：优化策略时冻结模型参数，但保留模型对状态和动作的导数。默认的 imagination horizon 是 32 步，再往后由 critic 补上，这样就不用把反传链拉得无限长。这里的 world model 是一个低维的动力学代理，不是生成视频的 WAM。可微带来了更直接的梯度，也带来了模型偏差，以及策略钻模型误差空子的风险。

The schematic omits termination/survival weights to highlight gradient flow: model parameters stay fixed while state/action derivatives remain active. A 32-step imagination horizon uses a critic for longer-term value. This is a low-dimensional dynamics surrogate, not a video-generating WAM. Pathwise gradients can be efficient but remain vulnerable to model error and exploitation.

它和 Deep RL 里的 Dyna、MBPO 一样，都是从真实采集到的状态出发，在模型里跑短 rollout。区别在于，MBPO 一般把合成的 transition 交给 model-free 算法去学，SuperMimic 则直接穿过模型求策略梯度。它和 ASAP 也不一样：ASAP 用真机反馈去对齐仿真，SuperMimic 的模型拟合的主要是 simulator 本身的动力学。

Like Dyna/MBPO, it starts short model rollouts from sampled states. MBPO typically hands synthetic transitions to a model-free learner, whereas SuperMimic differentiates through the model. ASAP instead uses real feedback to align simulation; SuperMimic primarily models simulator dynamics.

7Teleop 与 Sim2Real：对齐信息和动力学Teleoperation and sim-to-real alignment

人形 Teleop 远不只是把人手的位置发给机器人。输入可能只有头和双手的稀疏跟踪，也可能是全身 mocap；输出却必须同时满足脚的接触、平衡、关节限制和双臂的任务。人和机器人的身材比例不同，Retargeting 会改变末端的相对位置；控制器还得应付网络时延、输入抖动和做不到的指令。

Humanoid teleoperation ranges from sparse head/hand tracking to full-body mocap, yet outputs must satisfy support, balance, joint limits and manipulation. Retargeting changes geometry across bodies; controllers must also tolerate latency, noisy inputs and infeasible commands.

在仿真里训练时，teacher 或 critic 能看到精确的速度、外力和接触；上了真机，student 只能用实际测得到的观测。这一点一定要写清楚：训练时用了特权状态，不代表部署时也能拿到这些信息。History 和 state estimator，就是在看不全的情况下推断这些隐藏量的办法。

Simulation may expose exact velocities, forces and contacts to a teacher or critic, while a real student receives only measurable signals. Privileged training does not grant those signals at deployment. History and estimation infer hidden variables from limited observations.

ASAP：用真实反馈修正仿真ASAP: correct simulation using real feedback

faligned(s,a)=fsim ⁣(s,a+Δaϕ(s,a))f_{\mathrm{aligned}}(s,a)=f_{\mathrm{sim}}\!\left(s,a+\Delta a_\phi(s,a)\right)

ASAP 先把预训练的 tracking policy 部署到真机上采集轨迹，然后学一个在仿真里用的 delta-action model，让仿真的状态变化更接近真机记录；再把这个模型冻结，在修正过的仿真里继续训练 policy [67]。最后上真机的是微调后的策略，残差模型并不会一直在线修正真机的指令。

ASAP collects real rollouts, learns an action correction that makes simulation match those transitions, freezes it, then fine-tunes the policy inside the aligned simulator [67]. The final robot deploys the fine-tuned policy; the correction model is not an online residual controller on hardware.

这和大范围的 domain randomization 思路不同：随机化是想覆盖未知的变化，用真机反馈校准是想修正已经观测到的误差。两者可以一起用，但都替代不了对执行器饱和、时延和接触模型的检查。

Randomization covers possible variation; real-feedback calibration corrects observed mismatch. They can complement each other, but neither removes actuator limits, delays or contact-model validation.

8Loco-Manipulation：上下半身怎样协作Loco-manipulation: coordinating the whole body

机器人推门或者提重物时，手上受的力会影响全身的平衡，脚站在哪里又会影响手的精度。如果分别训练“会走”和“会抓”再拼起来，两个控制器很容易互相干扰。所以要想清楚：谁能看到全身的状态，谁来预测接触力，奖励怎么分配。

Opening a door or carrying a load couples hand forces to balance and foot placement to hand accuracy. Independently trained walking and grasping controllers may disturb each other. Specify shared observations, force handling and reward allocation.

FALCON 从 Proprioception 历史里隐式地感知受力，部署时 actor 并不直接接收外力；ULC 则靠统一的 command space 和手臂残差等设计降低训练难度。两条路线的区别不只是“一个网络还是两个网络”，更重要的是任务怎么拆、指令怎么分布、课程怎么排，这些都会改变学习问题本身。

FALCON’s actor responds to forces through proprioceptive history rather than direct force input. ULC simplifies unified learning through command design and arm residuals. The distinction is task decomposition, command distributions and curricula, not merely one network versus two.

9攀爬与多接触全身控制Climbing and whole-body control with multiple contacts

攀爬时，手也成了支撑点。爬梯子、爬墙和用微刺抓附，接触条件完全不同：摩擦锥、挂接方向、接触什么时候会失效、负载怎么分配，都要单独考虑，不能直接套用走路时的地面接触模型。参考动作也必须随环境几何调整，才能既保留动作结构，又保证接触可行。

Climbing makes hands support contacts. Ladders, walls and microspine attachment impose different friction, directional attachment, failure and load-sharing constraints. References must adapt to geometry while preserving viable contact structure.

LadderMan 从一条标好手脚接触时序的参考动作出发，先训练适配不同梯子几何的 state-based experts，再结合 DAgger 式的蒸馏和 PPO 得到视觉策略 [70]。Hybrid tracking 保留动作的骨架，但允许接触点随环境变化。爬到梯顶以后的操作用的是另一套控制器，所以这个系统虽然完整，却不是一个 policy 从头做到尾。

LadderMan starts from a reference with hand/foot contact timing, trains state-based experts across ladder geometries, then combines DAgger-style distillation and PPO for visual control [70]. Hybrid tracking preserves motion structure while adapting contacts. At the top, another controller handles manipulation; a unified system need not be a single policy.
